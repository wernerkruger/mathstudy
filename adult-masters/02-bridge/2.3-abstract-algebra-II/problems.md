# 2.3 Abstract Algebra II: Problems

Attempt each problem for at least 30 minutes before reading [solutions.md](solutions.md).

## Warm-up

**1.** Show that $\mathbb Z[i]$ is a Euclidean domain with respect to $N(a+bi) = a^2+b^2$. Find its units, and factor $2$, $3$ and $5$ into irreducibles in $\mathbb Z[i]$.

**2.** Show that the ideal $(2, x)$ in $\mathbb Z[x]$ is maximal but not principal. Conclude that $\mathbb Z[x]$ is not a PID.

**3.** Show that the following are irreducible over $\mathbb Q$:
(a) $x^4 + 1$; (b) $x^3 - 3x - 1$; (c) $x^4 + x + 1$.

**4.** Show that $[\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,\sqrt3):\mathbb Q] = 4$, that $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2,\sqrt3) = \mathbb Q(\sqrt2+\sqrt3)$, and find the minimal polynomial of $\sqrt2+\sqrt3$ over $\mathbb Q$.

**5.** List all abelian groups of order $72$ up to isomorphism, in both invariant factor and elementary divisor form.

**6.** Construct a field with 4 elements as a quotient of $\mathbb F_2[x]$. Write out its multiplication table and show its multiplicative group is cyclic.

## Core

**7.** Let $R$ be a PID. Show that every non-zero prime ideal of $R$ is maximal. Deduce that $F[x,y]$ ($F$ a field) is not a PID.

**8.** Let $G = \mathbb Z^3/N$, where $N$ is the subgroup generated by the columns of
$$
A = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 4 & 4 \\ -6 & 6 & 12 \\ 10 & -4 & -16\end{pmatrix}.
$$
Compute the Smith normal form of $A$ and identify $G$ in invariant factor form.

**9.** (a) A $6\times6$ complex matrix $A$ has characteristic polynomial $(x-2)^4(x-3)^2$ and minimal polynomial $(x-2)^2(x-3)$. List all possible Jordan canonical forms, and give the invariant factors (rational canonical form) in each case.
(b) Show that two $3\times 3$ matrices over a field $F$ are similar iff they have the same characteristic and minimal polynomials. Show this fails for $4\times4$ matrices.

**10.** Let $A\in M_n(\mathbb C)$ satisfy $A^m = I$ for some $m\ge1$. Show that $A$ is diagonalisable. Show by example that this fails over $\mathbb F_p$.

**11.** Let $\alpha\in\mathbb R$ be a root of $f(x) = x^3 - 3x - 1$.
(a) Show that $2 - \alpha^2$ is also a root of $f$, and deduce that $\mathbb Q(\alpha)$ is the splitting field of $f$ over $\mathbb Q$.
(b) Find $\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\alpha)/\mathbb Q)$, and confirm your answer using the discriminant.

**12.** Let $F$ be any field and $G$ a finite subgroup of $F^\times$. Show that $G$ is cyclic.

**13.** Show that $\mathbb F_{p^n}$ contains a subfield with $p^m$ elements iff $m\mid n$, and that such a subfield is unique.

**14.** (a) Show that $x^{p^n} - x$ is the product of all monic irreducible polynomials in $\mathbb F_p[x]$ whose degree divides $n$.
(b) Find the number of monic irreducible polynomials of degree $4$ over $\mathbb F_2$, and list them.

**15.** (a) Show that every irreducible polynomial over a field of characteristic $0$ is separable.
(b) Let $F = \mathbb F_p(t)$, the field of rational functions over $\mathbb F_p$. Show that $x^p - t\in F[x]$ is irreducible but not separable.

**16.** Let $\zeta = e^{2\pi i/5}$.
(a) Show that $[\mathbb Q(\zeta):\mathbb Q] = 4$ and $\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\zeta)/\mathbb Q)$ is cyclic of order 4.
(b) Find the unique subfield $E$ with $[E:\mathbb Q] = 2$, in the form $\mathbb Q(\sqrt d)$.

**17.** Show that the splitting field $K$ of $x^4 - 2$ over $\mathbb Q$ is $\mathbb Q(\sqrt[4]2, i)$, that $[K:\mathbb Q] = 8$, and that $\operatorname{Gal}(K/\mathbb Q)$ is the dihedral group of order 8.

**18.** Show that $\mathbb F_{p^n}/\mathbb F_p$ is Galois with cyclic Galois group of order $n$ generated by the Frobenius map $\varphi(a) = a^p$.

**19.** Show that the fields $\mathbb Q(\sqrt2)$ and $\mathbb Q(\sqrt3)$ are not isomorphic.

## Challenge

**20.** Show that $f(x) = x^5 - 6x + 3$ has Galois group $S_5$ over $\mathbb Q$, and conclude that the roots of $f$ cannot be expressed by radicals. (Prove the group-theoretic lemma you need: a $p$-cycle and a transposition generate $S_p$ for $p$ prime.)

**21.** Using the fact that every constructible real number has degree a power of $2$ over $\mathbb Q$, show that:
(a) the angle $60^\circ$ cannot be trisected with straightedge and compass;
(b) the regular 9-gon cannot be constructed with straightedge and compass.

**22.** (Kummer extensions.) Let $F$ be a field of characteristic $0$ containing a primitive $n$-th root of unity $\zeta$, let $a\in F$, and let $K = F(\beta)$ where $\beta^n = a$. Show that $K/F$ is Galois and that $\operatorname{Gal}(K/F)$ is cyclic of order dividing $n$.

**23.** (Primitive element theorem.) Let $F$ be an infinite field and $K = F(\alpha,\beta)$ a finite separable extension. Show that $K = F(\alpha + c\beta)$ for all but finitely many $c\in F$. Deduce that every finite separable extension of an infinite field is simple.

**24.** Let $R$ be a PID.
(a) Show that a finitely generated torsion-free $R$-module is free.
(b) Show that $\mathbb Q$ is a torsion-free $\mathbb Z$-module that is not free. What does this say about whether $\mathbb Q$ is finitely generated?
