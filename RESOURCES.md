## Standard paid textbooks worth owning

Buy these second-hand or borrow them from a library as you reach each phase.

- **Phase 1:** Velleman, *How to Prove It*; Abbott, *Understanding Analysis*; Dummit & Foote, *Abstract Algebra* (used again in Phases 2 and 3).
- **Phase 2:** Munkres, *Topology*; Stein & Shakarchi, *Complex Analysis*; Pugh, *Real Mathematical Analysis*; Needham, *Visual Complex Analysis*.
- **Phase 3:** Folland, *Real Analysis*; Brezis, *Functional Analysis, Sobolev Spaces and PDE*; Atiyah & Macdonald, *Introduction to Commutative Algebra*; Lee, *Introduction to Smooth Manifolds*.
- **Qualifying-exam practice:** de Souza & Silva, *Berkeley Problems in Mathematics*.

## Tools

- **LaTeX:** [Overleaf](https://www.overleaf.com/) (online) or a local TeX installation. Templates are in [templates/](templates/).
- **Flashcards:** [Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/).
- **Proof assistant (optional):** the Natural Number Game on the [Lean Game Server](https://adam.math.hhu.de/), then [*Mathematics in Lean*](https://leanprover-community.github.io/mathematics_in_lean/).
- **Questions:** Math StackExchange (math.stackexchange.com). MathOverflow (mathoverflow.net) is for research-level questions only.
