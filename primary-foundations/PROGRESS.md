# Things I can do now

[← Back to the track](README.md)

**For you:** this is your record of everything you've learned. When you can do something, write the date and add a sticker or draw a smiley. You decide when you're ready. There's no rush, and you can always come back and add things later.

When maths feels hard, look back here. Everything with a sticker was once something you couldn't do yet.

**For parents:** let him fill this in. Suggest a tick when you've seen him do something comfortably on two different days, but the decision is his. Print it, or keep it open on a tablet. Use the parent log at the bottom for your own notes.
