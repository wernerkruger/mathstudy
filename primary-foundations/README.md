# Primary foundations track: groep 6 to groep 8

[← Back to all tracks](../README.md)

For a 10-year-old in groep 6 (school year 2026–27) who has the basic skills but feels anxious or unsure about maths. The goal is to make maths feel safe and doable again, secure every groep 6–8 topic, and arrive at the brugklas confident and ready.

Material is in English with the Dutch school terms alongside. See the [glossary](GLOSSARY.md).

## Principles

The approach follows the research on maths anxiety summarised in the [parent guide](PARENT_GUIDE.md). In short:

1. **Short and often.** 15–20 minutes, 4–5 days a week. Stop while it is still going well.
2. **Mostly success.** Work at a level where about 70–80% of answers come out right. Confidence grows from competence.
3. **No time pressure.** No stopwatch drills or speed tests. Fluency comes after understanding, and it comes from games.
4. **Talk about how, not just the answer.** "How did you work it out? Is there another way?" Mistakes are information.
5. **Calm adults.** A parent's own worry about maths passes to children, especially during homework help. Games and the structured sheets here keep it calm.
6. **Concrete first.** Money, measuring in the kitchen, the number line (*getallenlijn*), strips and ratio tables (*verhoudingstabel*), the same models the school uses.

Each unit takes about 3–5 weeks at 15–20 minutes a day. The order follows the usual school sequence (De wereld in getallen and similar methods). Ask the teacher which method and pupil-tracking tests (LVS) the school uses and shift units to match what is happening in class: practising something a week or two before school teaches it is a great confidence boost.

## What each unit contains

Each unit is one folder with:

- `README.md`: what the unit is about, why it matters, Dutch terms, a "parent in 2 minutes" explanation, and "I can…" goals.
- `activities.md`: hands-on activities and games, and conversation prompts.
- `practice.md`: short practice sets, starting very easy and building up gently.
- `answers.md`: answers with brief explanations.

## Changes to know about

- New national goals for arithmetic and mathematics (*kerndoelen rekenen-wiskunde*) became law in August 2026. Schools can switch any time until August 2031. The content is mostly the same, with more emphasis on reasoning, estimating and checking, and reading graphs critically.
- The doorstroomtoets in groep 8 (about January–February 2029) tests arithmetic against fixed levels (1F, 1S/2F). It can only raise the school's advice, not lower it. There may be one national test by then; check in groep 7.
