# Resources

[← Back to the track](README.md)

All links were checked on 15 October 2026. Prices and free tiers change; check before you subscribe.

**A general note for an anxious child:** many apps reward speed with timers, streaks, coins or leaderboards. These can motivate some children and stress others. Sit with him the first few times, watch his face rather than the screen, and turn off or ignore timers where you can. If an app makes him tense, stop using it. Nothing on this list is essential.

## Dutch sites and apps (match school)

| Resource | What it is | Cost | Suits an anxious child? |
|---|---|---|---|
| [rekenen-oefenen.nl](https://www.rekenen-oefenen.nl/) ([groep 6 page](https://www.rekenen-oefenen.nl/rekenen-groep-6)) | Practice by groep and topic (splitsen, cijferen, breuken, kommagetallen, klokkijken, geld and more), with explanation videos. From Junior Einstein. | A limited number of free exercises a day; subscription for more. | **Good.** Topics match Dutch school terms. Use the explanation videos before the exercises. |
| [de-tafels-oefenen.nl](https://www.de-tafels-oefenen.nl/start) | Times-table practice per table, from the same publisher. | As above. | **Fine, with care.** Choose one table at a time; avoid any speed modes. |
| [Rekentuin / Prowise Learn](https://www.rekentuin.nl/) | Adaptive games used by many schools. | About €50 a year for a family; often free through school. | **Supervise.** Adapts to his level (good), but shows a visible timer and coins that reward speed. Some children find this stressful. Ask the school whether he already has a login. |
| [Sommenfabriek](https://www.sommenfabriek.nl/) | Free printable worksheets, with answers. | Free | **Good** for extra Warm-up sheets. Choose an easier level than his groep at first. |
| [Squla](https://www.squla.nl/) | Game-like practice for all subjects, matched to groep. | Paid; 7-day free trial. | **Reasonable.** Presents itself as "no performance pressure" (*geen prestatiedruk*). Try the trial and see. |
| [Schooltv: rekenen en wiskunde](https://schooltv.nl/onderwijsvorm/primair-onderwijs/rekenen-en-wiskunde) | Short free videos from Dutch public broadcasting. | Free | **Good.** Watch together; no scores or timers. |
| [Khan Academy (Dutch)](https://nl.khanacademy.org/) | Videos and exercises, partly translated. | Free | **Good.** No timers; mastery-based. Dutch coverage is patchy, and methods may differ from school's. |
| [Cito example materials for the doorstroomtoets](https://cito.nl/onderwijs/primair-onderwijs/leerling-in-beeld-doorstroomtoets/ontdek-de-doorstroomtoets/voorbeeldmateriaal/) | Example questions. | Free | **Groep 8 only, and gently.** Not needed in groep 6. |

## English-language resources

| Resource | What it is | Cost | Suits an anxious child? |
|---|---|---|---|
| [youcubed parent resources](https://www.youcubed.org/resource/parent-resources/) | Ideas, tasks and videos for parents from Jo Boaler's group at Stanford. | Free | **Very good** for parents. Low-pressure, visual tasks. |
| [NRICH (primary)](https://nrich.maths.org/students/primary) | Free, low-floor, high-ceiling problems from the University of Cambridge. Search by topic. | Free | **Very good** for the puzzle corner. Choose the easier ("one star") problems first. |
| [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/) | As above, in English. [Arithmetic](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/arithmetic) is the most relevant section. | Free | **Good.** Note it uses US methods for written calculation, which differ from Dutch *kolomsgewijs*. |
| [DragonBox](https://dragonbox.com/) | Puzzle apps that teach number sense and algebra through play. | Paid apps | **Very good.** Low stakes, no timers, very little text. |
| [Prodigy](https://www.prodigygame.com/) | Fantasy role-play game with maths questions. | Free core; paid membership. | **Mixed.** The battle format motivates some children and stresses others; heavy upselling. |
| [Beast Academy](https://beastacademy.com/online) | Challenging comic-style maths curriculum. | Paid | **Maybe later.** Excellent but demanding. Consider in groep 7–8 if his confidence grows and he enjoys puzzles. |
