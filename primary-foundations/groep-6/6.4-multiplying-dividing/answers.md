## Puzzle corner

- **Leaves 1 over for 2 and 3:** 7, 13, 19, 25, 31… (one more than a number in both the 2 and 3 tables: 6, 12, 18…).
- **Smallest for 2, 3, 4 and 5: 61 eggs.** 60 is the smallest number in the 2, 3, 4 and 5 tables, so 60 + 1 = 61.
- **And 6?** Yes: 61 : 6 = 10 rest 1.

Finding 7 or 13 is a real success. The jump to 61 is the "big challenge"; a helpful hint is "Which number is in the 4 table *and* the 5 table *and* the 3 table?"
