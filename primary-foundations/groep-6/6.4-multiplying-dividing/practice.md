# 6.4 Multiplying and dividing: practice

[← Unit overview](README.md) · [Answers](answers.md)

**For you:** splitting numbers into easy parts is the main trick in this unit. Write down your steps; they help you and they help anyone checking your work. No clock. At school, "divided by" is written with a colon: 92 : 4.

**For parents:** Set 4 item 9 uses the written method (*cijferend*). Skip it if school hasn't started it yet.

## Puzzle corner (optional)

A farmer counts her eggs.

- When she puts them in rows of **2**, there is **1** egg left over.
- In rows of **3**, there is **1** left over.
- In rows of **4**, there is **1** left over.
- In rows of **5**, there is **1** left over.

Start small: can you find a number that leaves 1 over when you divide by 2 **and** by 3?

*Big challenge:* what is the smallest number of eggs (more than 1) that works for 2, 3, 4 and 5? Does it work for 6 as well?
