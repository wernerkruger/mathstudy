# 6.6 First decimals: practice

[← Unit overview](README.md) · [Answers](answers.md)

**For you:** here we write decimals the Dutch way, with a comma: 0,5 and €2,45. (In English it's a dot: 0.5.) Money and a tape measure are great helpers. Think "cents" and "centimetres" whenever you get stuck. No clock.

## Puzzle corner (optional)

Use the digits **1, 2 and 3**, each once, and **one comma**, to make numbers. For example: 2,13 or 31,2.

- Make three different numbers. Which is the biggest?
- What is the **smallest** number you can make? The **biggest**?
- *Big challenge:* how many different numbers can you make? Put them all in order.

(The comma can't go at the very start or the very end.)
