## Puzzle corner

**12 numbers.** The comma can go after the first digit (six numbers) or after the second digit (six numbers). In order:

1,23 < 1,32 < 2,13 < 2,31 < 3,12 < 3,21 < 12,3 < 13,2 < 21,3 < 23,1 < 31,2 < 32,1

Smallest: **1,23**. Biggest: **32,1**. A neat observation: every number with the comma after two digits is bigger than every number with the comma after one digit.
