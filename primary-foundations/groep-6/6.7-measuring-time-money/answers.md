## Puzzle corner

With 24 cm of string, the two sides next to each other always add up to **12 cm** (half of 24). The rectangles with whole-centimetre sides are:

| Sides | Area |
|---|---|
| 1 × 11 | 11 cm² |
| 2 × 10 | 20 cm² |
| 3 × 9 | 27 cm² |
| 4 × 8 | 32 cm² |
| 5 × 7 | 35 cm² |
| 6 × 6 | 36 cm² |

Biggest area: the **square**, 6 × 6 = 36 cm². Smallest: the long thin 1 × 11 = 11 cm². The pattern: the closer to a square, the bigger the area. Spotting "the sides always add up to 12" is already a big insight.
