# 6.5 First fractions: practice

[← Unit overview](README.md) · [Answers](answers.md)

**For you:** keep your fraction strips next to you. You can fold, draw or use coins whenever you like. Here a fraction is written like 3/4: the bottom number (*noemer*) says how many equal parts the whole has; the top number (*teller*) says how many parts you have.

**For parents:** if fraction symbols cause worry, read the items aloud in words ("three quarters") and let him point or draw.

## Puzzle corner (optional)

A **unit fraction** has 1 on top: 1/2, 1/3, 1/4, 1/5…

- 1/2 + 1/2 = 1. Can you make 1 by adding **three** unit fractions? (They can be the same.)
- Can you make 1 using **different** unit fractions only, with no fraction used twice?
- *Big challenge:* how many different ways can you find? Use your strips to check.
