## Puzzle corner

- **Biggest sum: 1 173.** Put the biggest digits in the hundreds (6 and 5), then 4 and 3 in the tens, then 2 and 1 in the ones: for example 642 + 531 = 1 173. The order inside each column doesn't matter.
- **Closest to 1 000: 993.** For example 531 + 462 = 993. (Exactly 1 000 isn't possible with these digits.)
- **Smallest difference: 47.** 412 − 365 = 47. The trick is to make the hundreds digits next to each other (4 and 3), then make the top number's other digits as small as possible and the bottom number's as big as possible.

Any sum he makes is a success. The "biggest" question is the most approachable; "closest to 1 000" and "smallest difference" are real challenges.
