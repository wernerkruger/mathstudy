# 6.2 Times tables: practice

[← Unit overview](README.md) · [Answers](answers.md)

**For you:** there's no clock here, ever. Take as long as you like. Use any strategy. You can write down your thinking, use your fingers, or draw dots. Every answer you *work out* counts just as much as one you just *know*.

**For parents:** please don't time these. If he gets stuck, ask "What do you know that's close?" Set 1 is meant to feel easy.

## Set 2 · Building: doubling (*verdubbelen*)

Double the answer to go from ×2 to ×4, and again to go to ×8.

1. 2 × 6 = 12, so 4 × 6 = ____
2. 4 × 6 = 24, so 8 × 6 = ____
3. 2 × 7 = 14, so 4 × 7 = ____ and 8 × 7 = ____
4. 2 × 9 = 18, so 4 × 9 = ____
5. 4 × 5 = 20, so 8 × 5 = ____
6. 4 × 8 = ____ *(hint: 2 × 8 = 16)*
7. 8 × 3 = ____
8. 8 × 8 = ____
9. 4 × 4 = ____
10. 2 × 5 = ____

> **Talk about it:** Which was easier for you: 8 × 6 or 6 × 8? Are they the same?

## Set 3 · Building: ×10, ×5 and ×9

×5 is half of ×10. ×9 is ×10 minus one group.

1. 10 × 7 = ____, so 5 × 7 = ____
2. 10 × 8 = ____, so 5 × 8 = ____
3. 10 × 6 = ____, so 9 × 6 = ____
4. 10 × 7 = ____, so 9 × 7 = ____
5. 9 × 8 = ____
6. 9 × 9 = ____
7. 5 × 9 = ____
8. 6 × 5 = ____
9. 9 × 4 = ____
10. 10 × 9 = ____

> **Talk about it:** Look at your answers in the 9 table. Add the two digits of each answer together. What do you notice?

## Set 4 · Building: using a fact you know

Use a fact you know to work out the new one. Write your thinking if you like.

1. 3 × 7 = 2 × 7 + 1 × 7 = ____
2. 6 × 7 = 5 × 7 + 1 × 7 = ____
3. 7 × 7 = 6 × 7 + 7 = ____
4. 6 × 8 = 5 × 8 + 8 = ____
5. 7 × 8 = 5 × 8 + 2 × 8 = ____
6. 3 × 8 = ____
7. 6 × 6 = ____
8. 7 × 6 = ____
9. 6 × 9 = ____
10. 3 × 6 = ____
11. 7 × 3 = ____

> **Talk about it:** There are lots of ways to work out 7 × 8. Can you find two different ways?

## Set 5 · Stretch: dividing and word problems (*verhaaltjessommen*)

At school a division is written with a colon: 24 : 6 means 24 divided by 6. Think: "6 times what is 24?"

1. 24 : 6 = ____
2. 56 : 7 = ____
3. 42 : 6 = ____
4. 72 : 9 = ____
5. 63 : 7 = ____
6. ____ × 7 = 49
7. Eggs come in boxes of 6. You buy 7 boxes. How many eggs is that? ____
8. Renting a bike costs €9 a day. How much is it for 4 days? ____
9. You share 48 stickers fairly between 6 friends. How many stickers does each friend get? ____
10. Which is more, 6 × 8 or 7 × 7? By how much? ____
11. 5 × 5 = ____

## Puzzle corner (optional)

Look at a 10 × 10 times-table grid (rows 1 to 10, columns 1 to 10, with every answer filled in).

- The answer **12** appears in the grid 4 times: 2 × 6, 6 × 2, 3 × 4 and 4 × 3. Can you find another answer that appears 4 times?
- Can you find an answer that appears only **once**?
- *Big challenge:* the grid has 100 boxes. How many **different** answers are there?
