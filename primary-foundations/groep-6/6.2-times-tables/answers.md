## Puzzle corner

- **Answers that appear 4 times:** 6, 8, 10, 12, 18, 20, 24, 30 and 40. (For example 24 = 3 × 8 = 8 × 3 = 4 × 6 = 6 × 4.)
- **Answers that appear only once:** 1, 25, 49, 64, 81 and 100. They are all square numbers (*kwadraten*) that can't be made another way inside the grid.
- **Big challenge:** there are **42** different answers in the grid. A good way to check is to list every answer in order and tick it off. Finding the number exactly is hard; a careful, organised attempt is the real success here.
