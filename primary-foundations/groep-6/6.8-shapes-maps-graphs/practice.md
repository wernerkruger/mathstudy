## Puzzle corner (optional)

A cube net is 6 squares, joined edge to edge, that fold up into a cube. Nets that are just turned around or flipped over count as the same net.

- Find **one** cube net that isn't on this page.
- Find **three** different ones.
- *Big challenge:* how many different cube nets are there altogether? How can you be sure you've found them all?

Squared paper and scissors are your best tools here. This one can last a whole week.
