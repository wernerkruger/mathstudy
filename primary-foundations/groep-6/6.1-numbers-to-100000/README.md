# 6.1 Numbers to 100,000

[← Groep 6](../README.md) · [Activities](activities.md) · [Practice](practice.md) · [Answers](answers.md)

## What and why

In groep 6, numbers grow from 10,000 to 100,000. This unit is about understanding what each digit in a big number is worth (place value), placing numbers on a number line, and rounding. Almost everything else this year (written adding and subtracting, estimating, decimals) depends on a secure feel for place value, so it's a good, gentle place to start.

## Parent in 2 minutes

- **Place-value columns.** School uses the letters TD, D, H, T, E above digits (ten thousands, thousands, hundreds, tens, ones). Use the same letters at home.
- **Writing big numbers.** Dutch schools often write 45 302 with a space, or 45.302 with a full stop. A full stop is a thousands separator in Dutch, not a decimal point. In the practice sheets we use a space, which works in both languages.
- **Reading big numbers in Dutch** is tricky: 45 302 is *vijfenveertigduizend driehonderdtwee*, with ones before tens ("five-and-forty"). If he writes 54 302 when you say "forty-five thousand…", the Dutch word order may be the reason, not a lack of understanding.
- **The number line** (*getallenlijn*) is the school's main model. They use empty number lines (just a line with a few marked numbers) to place numbers, count in jumps and estimate.
- **Rounding.** Look at the digit to the right of the place you're rounding to: 5 or more rounds up, 4 or less rounds down. School often teaches this with the number line: "Which round number is it closer to?" Use the number line first and the rule second.

## Common stumbling blocks and what to say

| Stumbling block | What to say or do |
|---|---|
| Writes "three thousand and six" as 30006 or 306 | Use a place-value table (TD D H T E) and fill in a zero for every empty column. "Are there any hundreds? No? Then we write a 0 there." |
| Mixes up digit and value: "the 4 in 45 302 is worth 4" | "The digit is 4, but it's in the ten-thousands column. So it's worth 40 000. Like four €10,000 bags of money." |
| Rounds 6 480 to 7 000 because "8 is big" | "Which digit do we look at when rounding to thousands? The hundreds. There are 4 hundreds. Is 6 480 closer to 6 000 or 7 000?" Draw the number line. |
| Gets lost counting past a "round" boundary (39 900 → 39 1000) | Use money or a car's kilometre counter. "What happens when the hundreds are full?" |
| Freezes at very big numbers | Cover all but the last three digits, then reveal. Big numbers are just small numbers with more columns. |

## I can…

- read and write numbers up to 100,000
- say what each digit is worth (TD, D, H, T, E)
- put numbers on a number line
- round numbers to the nearest 10, 100 or 1000
- put big numbers in order from small to big
