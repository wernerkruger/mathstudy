## Puzzle corner

**24 numbers.** To round to 30 000, a number must be from 25 000 up to 34 999.

- Starting with **2**: the next digit must be 5 (25…), then 1, 3, 4 in any order: 6 numbers (25 134, 25 143, 25 314, 25 341, 25 413, 25 431).
- Starting with **3**: the next digit can be 1, 2 or 4, but not 5 (35… is too big and rounds to 40 000). For each, the other three digits can go in 6 orders: 3 × 6 = 18 numbers.
- Total 6 + 18 = 24.

Any correct number he finds is a success. Listing them systematically (in order) is the "big challenge" skill, so praise an organised list even if it isn't complete.
