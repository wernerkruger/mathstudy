## Puzzle corner

Mix A is stronger. In Mix A, 2 of the 5 cups are syrup (2/5 = 16/40); in Mix B, 3 of the 8 cups are syrup (3/8 = 15/40). Close, so tasting may not settle it, which is why the maths is useful. Mix C is a genuinely hard question; trying is the point. A mix *somewhere in between* is easy to find: pour Mix A and Mix B together (5 cups syrup, 8 cups water, so 5/13 syrup). *Exactly* halfway is 31/80 syrup (halfway between 32/80 and 30/80), so 31 cups of syrup with 49 cups of water: a very big jug.
