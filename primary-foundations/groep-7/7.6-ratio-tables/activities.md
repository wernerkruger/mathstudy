# 7.6 Activities: ratio tables

[← Unit 7.6](README.md) · [Practice](practice.md) · [Games](../../GAMES.md)

## 1. Pancake party

**You need:** a pancake recipe (a common Dutch one: 250 g flour, 500 ml milk, 2 eggs for about 4 people), the kitchen.

**How:** Decide how many people are coming (say 6, or 10). He makes a ratio table for each ingredient, works out the amounts, then measures them and you cook together. Eating the result is part of the maths.

**Talk about it:** "Which step did you use to get from 4 to 6 people? Could you have done it differently?"

## 2. Supermarket detective

**You need:** a supermarket visit or an online shop.

**How:** Pick a product that comes in two sizes (peanut butter, cereal, juice). Note size and price for both. At home, make a ratio table for each and compare the price for the same amount (per 100 g or per kilo). Then check the shelf label: Dutch supermarkets usually show the price per kilo or litre in small print. Did he get the same answer?

**Talk about it:** "Is the bigger pack always cheaper per kilo? Is the cheaper-per-kilo pack always the better buy for us?"

## 3. Bike-ride speed

**You need:** a bike ride, a bike computer or phone app showing distance, a watch.

**How:** On a ride, note the distance and time (for example 6 km in 24 minutes). At home, make a ratio table to work out how far you'd go in an hour at that speed. That's your speed in km per hour.

| Minutes | 24 | 12 | 60 |
|---|---|---|---|
| km | 6 | 3 | ? |

**Talk about it:** "Did we ride at exactly that speed the whole time? What does 'average speed' mean?"

## 4. Lemonade mix

**You need:** syrup (*siroop* or *ranja*), water, a measuring jug, small cups.

**How:** The bottle says 1 part syrup to 5 parts water (check your bottle). Make 600 ml of lemonade correctly using a ratio table. Then make a "too weak" and a "too strong" version and taste-test them.

**Talk about it:** "If we use 2 parts syrup to 10 parts water, does it taste the same? Why?"

## 5. Ratio table relay

Play "Ratio table relay" from [GAMES.md](../../GAMES.md): take turns adding one column to the same table, saying the move out loud ("I'm doubling").

## Conversation prompts for any day

- "We're going 30 km to grandma's at 60 km per hour on the small roads. How long will it take?"
- "This 6-pack costs € 4,20. What does one bottle cost? Is that cheaper than the shop's single bottle?"
- "Can you think of two things that are *not* in proportion?" (Age and height: double the age doesn't mean double the height.)
