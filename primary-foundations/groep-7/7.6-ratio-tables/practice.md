# 7.6 Practice: ratio tables (*verhoudingstabel*)

[← Unit 7.6](README.md) · [Answers](answers.md)

Copy the tables onto paper and fill in the gaps. Write your step above each new column (×2, :2, ×10, +). There is often more than one good route. Any route that gets there is fine.

---

## Set 1 · Warm-up: the moves

**A.** Eggs come in packs of 6.

| Packs | 1 | 2 | 4 | 10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Eggs | 6 | 1. ? | 2. ? | 3. ? |

**B.** A swimming-pool ticket costs € 4.

| Tickets | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price (€) | 4 | 4. ? | 5. ? |

**C.** Apples: 2 kg cost € 3.

| kg | 2 | 4 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price (€) | 3 | 6. ? | 7. ? |

**D.** A box of 10 pens costs € 8.

| Pens | 10 | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price (€) | 8 | 8. ? | 9. ? |

10. One bike has 2 wheels. How many wheels do 5 bikes have?

> **Talk about it:** In table C, how did you get from 2 kg to 1 kg?

## Set 2 · Building: recipes

Pancakes (*pannenkoeken*) for 4 people: 250 g flour, 500 ml milk, 2 eggs.

| People | 4 | 8 | 2 | 6 | 12 | 10 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Flour (g) | 250 | 1. ? | | 5. ? | | 8. ? | |
| Milk (ml) | 500 | 2. ? | 4. ? | 6. ? | | | 9. ? |
| Eggs | 2 | | 3. ? | | 7. ? | | |

10. How many eggs do you need for 4 people?

> **Talk about it:** How did you find the amount for 6 people? Did anyone in your family do it a different way?

## Set 3 · Building: prices

1. 3 croissants cost € 2,40. What do 6 croissants cost?
2. What does 1 croissant cost?
3. What do 9 croissants cost?
4. 500 g of cheese costs € 6. What does 1 kg cost?
5. What does 250 g cost?
6. What does 750 g cost?
7. At the market, 100 g of sweets (*snoep*) costs € 1,20. What does 350 g cost?
8. Which is cheaper per 100 g: a 400 g bag of nuts for € 3,20, or a 250 g bag for € 2,25?
9. Bananas cost € 1,80 per kilo. You buy 1,5 kg. What do you pay?
10. 1 kg of potatoes costs € 2. What do 2 kg cost?

> **Talk about it:** For question 8, what did you make the same in both tables?

## Set 4 · Building: speed (*snelheid*)

Lisa cycles at 15 km per hour.

| Time | 1 hour | 2 hours | 1/2 hour | 3 hours | 1 1/2 hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Distance (km) | 15 | 1. ? | 2. ? | 3. ? | 4. ? |

5. At 15 km per hour, how long does Lisa take to cycle 60 km?

Jesse walks at 6 km per hour.

6. How far does he walk in 30 minutes?
7. How far does he walk in 10 minutes?

A train travels at 120 km per hour.

8. How far does it go in 15 minutes?
9. How many minutes does it take to go 90 km?

10. A car drives at 100 km per hour for 2 hours. How far does it go?

> **Talk about it:** Why is it easier to use 30, 15 or 10 minutes than 25 minutes?

## Set 5 · Stretch: mixed problems

1. 1 litre of paint covers 8 m² of wall. How many litres do you need for 20 m²?
2. A car uses 1 litre of petrol for every 20 km. How many litres for 150 km?
3. Lemonade: 1 part syrup to 5 parts water. You make 1,2 litres of lemonade. How much syrup do you need? (Give your answer in ml.)
4. A recipe for 6 people needs 300 g pasta. How much pasta for 9 people?
5. A printer prints 24 pages in 3 minutes. How long does it take to print 40 pages?
6. 5 cinema tickets cost € 37,50. What do 7 tickets cost?
7. Ruben cycles 9 km in 30 minutes. Sara cycles 12 km in 45 minutes. Who cycles faster? (Hint: work out how far each would go in 60 minutes.)
8. Two lollipops cost € 1. What do six cost?

> **Talk about it:** Choose one problem and explain your route to someone at home. Did they choose the same route?

## Puzzle corner (optional)

**Taste test.** Mix A is 2 cups of syrup with 3 cups of water. Mix B is 3 cups of syrup with 5 cups of water.

- Which mix tastes more of syrup? Guess first.
- Make both with a small cup and taste them (if you have syrup at home).
- Can you prove it with a ratio table or with fractions? (Hint: what fraction of each whole jug is syrup?)
- Invent a Mix C that is exactly halfway in strength. Is that easy or hard?
