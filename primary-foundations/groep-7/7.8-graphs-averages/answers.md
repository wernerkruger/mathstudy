## Set 2 · Building: line graph

1. 15:00 (18 °C)
2. 8 degrees (16 − 8)
3. 7 degrees (18 − 11)
4. Between 09:00 and 12:00 (5 degrees; the line is steepest there)
5. 10 degrees (18 − 8)

7. 09:00 and 21:00 (both 11 °C)

Talk about it: a line graph shows how something changes over time. A pie chart shows parts of one whole, and temperatures aren't parts of anything.

## Puzzle corner

- Five numbers with average 6 and no 6: the total must be 30, for example 5, 5, 5, 7, 8 or 1, 2, 3, 4, 20.
- All different and below 10: for example 2, 4, 7, 8, 9 (total 30).
- No. The average is "levelling out", so it is always between the smallest and the largest number (or equal to them if all the numbers are the same).
- You need a 9. To average 7,5 over four tests the total must be 30; after three tests averaging 7 the total is 21; 30 − 21 = 9.
