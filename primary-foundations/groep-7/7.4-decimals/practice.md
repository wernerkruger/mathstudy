## Puzzle corner (optional)

**Make 1.** Find three *different* kommagetallen with one digit after the comma (like 0,2 or 0,7) that add up to exactly 1. For example, is 0,1 + 0,2 + 0,7 allowed?

- Find another way.
- How many different ways can you find? (0,1 + 0,2 + 0,7 and 0,7 + 0,2 + 0,1 count as the same.)
- How do you know when you've found them all?
