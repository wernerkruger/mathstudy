5. a. Finn (3,8 m = 3,80 m) b. 3,08 m (Noor) · 3,48 m (Mila) · 3,8 m (Finn)

Talk about it: compare from the left. 3,7 has 7 tenths, 3,698 has only 6 tenths.

## Puzzle corner

There are exactly 4 ways: 0,1 + 0,2 + 0,7 · 0,1 + 0,3 + 0,6 · 0,1 + 0,4 + 0,5 · 0,2 + 0,3 + 0,5. It's the same puzzle as "three different whole numbers that add up to 10". A tidy way to be sure you have them all: start with the smallest number first (0,1, then 0,2…) and keep the three numbers in order from small to large.
