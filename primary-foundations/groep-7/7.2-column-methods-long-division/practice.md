## Puzzle corner (optional)

**Digit cards.** Use the digits 1, 2, 3 and 4 once each to make a two-digit × two-digit sum: ▢▢ × ▢▢. Which arrangement gives the biggest answer? Which gives the smallest? Can you explain why, without trying them all?

**Remainder detective.** Find a number between 1 and 50 that leaves remainder 1 when you divide it by 2, by 3 *and* by 4. Can you find more than one? Do you see a pattern?
