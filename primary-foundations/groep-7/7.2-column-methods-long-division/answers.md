## Set 5 · Stretch: remainders and kommagetallen

1. 6 rest 1

6. 125

Talk about it: buses round up (everyone must go), money keeps dividing into cents, egg boxes count only full ones and say what's left.

## Puzzle corner

- **Digit cards:** biggest is 41 × 32 = 1 312 (just ahead of 42 × 31 = 1 302). The big digits go in the tens places; then it's worth trying both ways. Smallest is 13 × 24 = 312 (small digits in the tens places, and again try both ways: 14 × 23 = 322).
- **Remainder detective:** 13, 25, 37 and 49 (and 1, if you allow 1 : 2 = 0 rest 1). They go up in steps of 12, because 12 is the smallest number that 2, 3 and 4 all divide into.
