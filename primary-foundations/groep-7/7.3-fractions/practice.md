4. 4/12

## Puzzle corner (optional)

**Ancient Egyptian fractions.** The ancient Egyptians liked to write fractions as sums of *different* unit fractions (*stambreuken*): fractions with 1 on top.

- Write 3/4 as the sum of two different unit fractions.
- Write 5/6 as the sum of two different unit fractions.
- Can you write 1 as the sum of three different unit fractions? Is there more than one way?
- Make up your own and test it on someone at home.
