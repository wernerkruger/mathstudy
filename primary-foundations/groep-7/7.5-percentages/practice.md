# 7.5 Practice: percentages (*procenten*)

[← Unit 7.5](README.md) · [Answers](answers.md)

Remember the friendly percentages: 100% is everything, 50% is half, 25% is a quarter, 10% is a tenth, 1% is a hundredth. You can draw a bar (*strook*) or a ratio table (*verhoudingstabel*) for any question.

## Set 4 · Building: discount (*korting*)

For each one, work out how much you save *and* the new price.

1. A T-shirt costs € 20. Korting: 50%.
2. Trainers cost € 60. Korting: 25%.
3. A computer game costs € 40. Korting: 10%.
4. A bike costs € 250. Korting: 20%.
5. A winter jacket costs € 80. Korting: 30%.
6. Ice skates cost € 45. Korting: 10%.
7. Packs of stroopwafels cost € 3 each. Offer: *2e halve prijs* (the second pack is half price).
   a. What do two packs cost?
   b. What percentage do you save on the two packs together?
8. A 400 g pack of muesli has "25% extra gratis". How many grams are in the pack now?
9. A book costs € 10. Korting: 50%. What do you pay?

> **Talk about it:** In question 8, is "25% extra" the same as "25% korting"?

## Puzzle corner (optional)

**The swap trick.** Which is bigger: 10% of 50, or 50% of 10?

- Try a few more pairs: 20% of 5 and 5% of 20. 25% of 8 and 8% of 25.
- What do you notice? Does it always work?
- Use it to work out 8% of 25 in your head the easy way.
