# 7.5 Percentages (*procenten*)

[← Groep 7](../README.md) · [Activities](activities.md) · [Practice](practice.md) · [Answers](answers.md) · [Parent guide](../../PARENT_GUIDE.md) · [Games](../../GAMES.md) · [Glossary](../../GLOSSARY.md)

## What and why

Percent means "out of a hundred" (*per honderd*). In groep 7 he learns what a percentage means, how to work out the "friendly" percentages (50%, 25%, 10%, 1%) of an amount, how percentages link to fractions (50% = 1/2), and simple discount (*korting*) sums. Percentages are among the most useful pieces of maths in daily life: sales, phone batteries, test scores, the weather forecast. The new national goals specifically mention everyday contexts like pocket money and discounts.

## Parent in 2 minutes

**The bar (strook).** Dutch methods use a bar model: a long strip with 0% to 100% along the top and the real amount (for example € 0 to € 80) along the bottom. To find 25%, you look at a quarter of the bar. The bar shows that "100%" is always "the whole thing", whatever it is.

**Friendly percentages first.** Everything is built from a few anchors:
- 100% = everything
- 50% = half (: 2)
- 25% = a quarter (: 4, or half of half)
- 10% = a tenth (: 10)
- 1% = a hundredth (: 100)

Then combine: 20% = 2 × 10%; 5% = half of 10%; 75% = 50% + 25%; 15% = 10% + 5%. School often writes this in a ratio table (*verhoudingstabel*) with % on the top row and the amount on the bottom row. Let him use whichever he prefers.

**Discount (*korting*).** Two steps: work out the discount, then subtract it from the price. "Shirt € 40, 25% korting: 25% of € 40 = € 10, new price € 30." A shortcut for later: 25% korting means you pay 75%. Don't push the shortcut; the two-step version is easier to understand and check.

**Percentages and fractions.** 50% = 1/2, 25% = 1/4, 75% = 3/4, 10% = 1/10, 20% = 1/5, 1% = 1/100. In groep 8 this is extended to decimals (0,25 = 25%).

**No calculator.** In groep 7 all percentage sums are done in the head or on paper with friendly numbers. The calculator comes in groep 8.

## Common stumbling blocks and what to say

| What you might see | What to say or do |
|---|---|
| Thinks 10% of 70 is 10 | "10% means 10 out of every 100. Here we don't have 100, we have 70. What's a tenth of 70?" |
| Thinks 50% is always 50 | "50% of 200 kids? 50% of 10 sweets?" Use real objects. |
| Forgets the second step in korting (gives the discount as the answer) | "Is that what you save, or what you pay? Read the question again." Draw the bar: the discount bit and the "pay" bit. |
| Unsure with 1/3 = 33% | "1/3 doesn't come out exactly. It's 33 and a third percent. We say *about* 33%." |
| Believes 50% off and then another 50% off makes it free | Try it with € 100 and see. (Nice thinking-critically moment.) |

## "I can…" goals

- I can explain what "percent" means.
- I can work out 50%, 25%, 10% and 1% of an amount.
- I can use those to work out other percentages, like 20%, 5% and 75%.
- I can change easy percentages into fractions and back.
- I can work out the new price after a korting.
- I can check whether a "deal" in a shop really is a good deal.
