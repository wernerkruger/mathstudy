# Glossary: Dutch–English maths terms (groep 6–8)

[← Back to the track](README.md)

Organised by the four domains used in Dutch primary schools and on pupil-tracking tests: Numbers (*Getallen*), Ratios (*Verhoudingen*), Measurement and Geometry (*Meten en Meetkunde*) and Relationships (*Verbanden*). The last column shows when the term usually first appears.

**Note on notation:** Dutch schools write a decimal comma (3,5) where English uses a decimal point (3.5), and often a full stop or space to group thousands (12.500 or 12 500). This track uses the English style in parent text and gives the Dutch style in child-facing practice where it matters. Point this out to him: "At school they write 3,5. In English it's 3.5. It's the same number."
