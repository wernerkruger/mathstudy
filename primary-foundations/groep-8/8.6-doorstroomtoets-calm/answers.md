# 8.6 Answers: test-style mixed sets

[← Unit overview](README.md) · [Practice](practice.md)

For the parent: check together, and start with what went well. For each wrong answer, ask "Which step slipped?" and note the topic. If one topic keeps slipping, go back to that unit for a few sessions rather than doing more mixed sets. The level (1F or 1S) is an estimate of which level each question mainly tests.

## Set 1 (1F)

1. **623.** [G, 1F] Column addition: 5 + 8 = 13, carry 1.
2. **543.** [G, 1F] Count on from 457: +3 → 460, +40 → 500, +500 → 1 000.
3. **360.** [G, 1F] 24 × 10 = 240, 24 × 5 = 120; 240 + 120.
4. **250 cm.** [M, 1F] 1 m = 100 cm.
5. **18 juice boxes.** [V, 1F] 3 × 6.
6. **108.** [G, 1F] 800 : 8 = 100, 64 : 8 = 8.
7. **5 children.** [Vb, 1F] Count the squares.
8. **€4,50.** [V, 1F] Divide by 10.
9. **B. 90°.** [M, 1F]
10. **C. 0,8.** [G, 1F] Compare as 0,800 / 0,750 / 0,090 / 0,079.

## Set 2 (1F)

1. **6,25.** [G, 1F] 3,50 + 2,75.
2. **15.** [V, 1F] 60 : 4.
3. **1,25 kg.** [M, 1F] 1 000 g = 1 kg.
4. **1 hour 35 minutes.** [M, 1F] 19:45 → 20:45 → 21:20 is 1 h + 35 min.
5. **Area 24 m², perimeter 20 m.** [M, 1F] 6 × 4; 6 + 4 + 6 + 4.
6. **a. Tuesday (di). b. 6 degrees. c. 12 °C.** [Vb, 1F] 15 − 9 = 6; (12 + 15 + 9 + 14 + 10) : 5 = 60 : 5.
7. **8:57.** [Vb, 1F] The buses are 15 minutes apart.
8. **2 500.** [G, 1F] Tens digit 8 → round up.
9. **1 500 ml.** [M, 1F]
10. **22 children; 5 more.** [Vb, 1F] 8 + 5 + 3 + 6 = 22; 8 − 3 = 5.

## Set 3 (1F → 1S)

1. **60%.** [V, 1F] 1/5 = 20%, so 3/5 = 60%.
2. **B. 1/8.** [V, 1S] 1/8 = half of 0,25 = 0,125.
3. **€48.** [V, 1F] 25% of €64 = €16; €64 − €16.
4. **1,5 km.** [M, 1S] 1 cm = 500 m; 3 × 500 m = 1 500 m.
5. **60 km/u.** [V, 1S] 90 km in 1,5 h → 30 km in 0,5 h → 60 km in 1 h.
6. **5/6.** [G, 1S] 2/3 = 4/6; 4/6 + 1/6.
7. **8.** [G, 1S] Same as 72 : 9 (multiply both by 10).
8. **The 1 kg bag.** [V, 1S] 750 g for €3,00 → 250 g for €1,00 → 1 kg for €4,00. The 1 kg bag is €3,80.
9. **72 litres.** [M, 1S] 60 × 30 × 40 = 72 000 cm³; 1 000 cm³ = 1 l.
10. **€60,50.** [V, 1S] 21% of €50 = €10 + €0,50 = €10,50; €50 + €10,50.

## Set 4 (1S stretch)

1. **25%.** [V, 1S] €10 more out of the original €40 = ¼.
2. **0,6 · 65% · 2/3.** [G, 1S] As decimals: 0,60, 0,65, 0,666…
3. **500 g.** [V, 1F] 50 g per person; 10 × 50 g.
4. **70°.** [M, 1S] 180 − 35 − 75.
5. **8.** [Vb, 1F] 6 + 8 + 7 + 9 + 10 = 40; 40 : 5.
6. **4 500.** [G, 1F] × 1 000: move three places.
7. **a. 20 km/u. b. 20 km.** [Vb, 1S] 5 km every 15 minutes; 4 × 15 min = 1 hour, so 4 × 5 km.
8. **€18.** [Vb, 1S] €4 + 7 × €2 = €4 + €14.
9. **1 000 litres.** [M, 1S] 1 m³ = 1 000 dm³ and 1 dm³ = 1 l.
10. **50.** [V, 1F] Half of 100.

## Puzzle corner

1. **Any questions that fit the domain.** Check together that each one has one clear answer.
2. **½ of the time.** One of the two left is right.
3. **40% of two hours.** 40% of 120 minutes = 48 minutes; ¾ of an hour = 45 minutes.
