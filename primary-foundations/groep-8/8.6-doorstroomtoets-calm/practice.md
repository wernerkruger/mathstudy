# 8.6 Practice: test-style mixed sets

[← Unit overview](README.md) · [Answers](answers.md)

These four sets look like doorstroomtoets questions: different topics mixed together, some open questions and some multiple choice. Each question has a small label for its domain:

- **[G]** *Getallen* (numbers)
- **[V]** *Verhoudingen* (ratios, percentages, fractions)
- **[M]** *Meten en Meetkunde* (measuring and geometry)
- **[Vb]** *Verbanden* (tables, graphs, rules)

**There is no clock.** Take your time. Use your toolkit: breathe, read twice, skip and come back, draw it, estimate to check. Scrap paper is always allowed.

Sets 1 and 2 are at the fundamental level (1F). Sets 3 and 4 reach towards the target level (1S). If Sets 3 and 4 feel hard, that's normal: they're a stretch.

---

## Set 1 (1F)

1. **[G]** 345 + 278 = …
2. **[G]** 1 000 − 457 = …
3. **[G]** 24 × 15 = …
4. **[M]** 2,5 m = … cm
5. **[V]** Juice boxes are sold in packs of 6. You buy 3 packs. How many juice boxes do you have?
6. **[G]** 864 : 8 = …
7. **[Vb]** Groep 8 voted for their favourite fruit.

   ```
   appel        ■■■■■■■■
   banaan       ■■■■■
   peer         ■■■
   sinaasappel  ■■■■■■
   (■ = 1 child)
   ```

   How many children chose *banaan*?
8. **[V]** How much is 10% of €45?
9. **[M]** How many degrees is a right angle (*rechte hoek*)?
   A. 45°  B. 90°  C. 180°  D. 360°
10. **[G]** Which number is the biggest?
    A. 0,75  B. 0,09  C. 0,8  D. 0,079

## Set 2 (1F)

1. **[G]** 3,5 + 2,75 = …
2. **[V]** How much is ¼ of 60?
3. **[M]** 1 250 g = … kg
4. **[M]** A film starts at 19:45 and ends at 21:20. How long is the film?
5. **[M]** A garden is a rectangle of 6 m by 4 m. What is its area (*oppervlakte*)? What is its perimeter (*omtrek*)?
6. **[Vb]** The temperature at noon this week:

   | Day | ma | di | wo | do | vr |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | °C | 12 | 15 | 9 | 14 | 10 |

   a. On which day was it warmest?
   b. How many degrees difference between the warmest and the coldest day?
   c. What was the average temperature?
7. **[Vb]** A bus leaves the stop at 8:12, 8:27 and 8:42. It keeps going like this. When does the next bus after 8:42 leave?
8. **[G]** Round 2 486 to the nearest hundred.
9. **[M]** 1,5 l = … ml
10. **[Vb]** Look at the fruit chart in Set 1, question 7. How many children voted altogether? How many more chose *appel* than *peer*?

---

## Set 3 (1F → 1S)

1. **[V]** Write 3/5 as a percentage.
2. **[V]** Which fraction is the same as 0,125?
   A. 1/125  B. 1/8  C. 1/4  D. 12/5
3. **[V]** A pair of trainers costs €64. There is 25% korting. What is the new price?
4. **[M]** On a map with scale 1 : 50 000, two churches are 3 cm apart. How far apart are they in real life, in km?
5. **[V]** A train travels 90 km in 1,5 hours. What is its average speed in km/u?
6. **[G]** 2/3 + 1/6 = …
7. **[G]** 7,2 : 0,9 = …
8. **[V]** Rice: a 750 g bag costs €3,00 and a 1 kg bag costs €3,80. Which is cheaper per kilo?
9. **[M]** An aquarium is 60 cm long, 30 cm wide and 40 cm high. How many litres of water does it hold when full?
10. **[V]** A bike repair costs €50 *excl. btw*. Btw is 21%. What do you pay *incl. btw*?

---

## Set 4 (1S stretch, finishing on a win)

1. **[V]** A family cinema ticket used to cost €40. Now it costs €50. By what percentage did the price go up?
2. **[G]** Put in order, smallest first: 2/3 · 0,6 · 65%
3. **[V]** A recipe for 4 people uses 200 g rice. How much rice for 10 people?
4. **[M]** A triangle has angles of 35° and 75°. How big is the third angle?
5. **[Vb]** Five test scores: 6, 8, 7, 9, 10. What is the average?
6. **[G]** 4,5 × 1 000 = …
7. **[Vb]** The table shows how far Yara has cycled.

   | Time (minutes) | 0 | 15 | 30 | 45 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|
   | Distance (km) | 0 | 5 | 10 | 15 |

   a. What is her speed in km/u?
   b. If she keeps going at the same speed, how far will she have cycled after 1 hour?
8. **[Vb]** A taxi costs €4 to start plus €2 per km. What does a 7 km ride cost?
9. **[M]** 1 m³ = … litres
10. **[V]** How much is 50% of 100?

**Talk about it (after any set):** Which question did you star as hard? Was it as hard as it looked? Which part of your toolkit helped most?

## Puzzle corner (optional, just for fun)

1. Make up your own test question for each of the four domains. Give them to your parent to solve.
2. A multiple-choice question has four answers. You know for sure that two of them are wrong. What fraction of the time would a guess between the other two be right?
3. Which is more: 3/4 of an hour or 40% of two hours?
