# Groep 8 (2028–29): pulling it together, calmly

[← Back to the track overview](../README.md) · [Parent guide](../PARENT_GUIDE.md) · [Games](../GAMES.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) · [Resources](../RESOURCES.md) · [Progress tracker](../PROGRESS.md)

Groep 8 is the last year of primary school (*basisschool*). Almost nothing in it is brand new. Most of the year is about joining up what your son already knows: seeing that ½, 0,5 and 50% are the same number, using a ratio table (*verhoudingstabel*) for scale and speed, and solving longer word problems (*redactiesommen* or *verhaaltjessommen*) one step at a time. The year also has two moments that can feel big to an anxious child: the national end-of-primary test (*doorstroomtoets*) around January–February 2029, and the move to secondary school (*brugklas*) in September 2029.

The aim of this folder is simple: he walks into the doorstroomtoets having seen every type of question before, and walks into the brugklas knowing he can do maths.

> **A note on commas.** Dutch schools write decimals with a comma: 2,5 rather than 2.5, and €3,50. The child-facing practice sheets use the comma, as school does. Calculators and some apps show a point; unit 8.5 explains this.

## The four domains

The national levels for arithmetic (*referentieniveaus rekenen*) group all primary maths into four domains (*domeinen*). The doorstroomtoets and the school's tracking tests (*leerlingvolgsysteem*, LVS) report on them separately, so it helps to know the names ([Boom on referentieniveaus](https://boom.nl/primair-onderwijs/artikelen/80-3135_Vier-dingen-over-referentieniveaus-die-je-moet-weten)):

| Domain | In plain words | Units |
|---|---|---|
| **Getallen** (numbers) | Place value, the four operations, fractions and decimals as numbers, rounding, estimating | 8.1, 8.5, 8.7 |
| **Verhoudingen** (ratios) | Ratio tables, percentages, scale, speed, "best buy" | 8.1, 8.2 |
| **Meten en Meetkunde** (measuring and geometry) | Length, area, volume, weight, time, money, angles, shapes, maps | 8.3 |
| **Verbanden** (relationships) | Reading and making tables, charts and graphs; spotting patterns and rules | 8.4, 8.5, 8.6, 8.7 |

**The two levels.** Level **1F** is the fundamental level that at least 85% of children should reach by the end of groep 8. Level **1S** (*streefniveau*, roughly the same as 2F) is the higher target. This programme aims to make 1F feel solid and comfortable, then reach towards 1S. Nobody needs to reach 1S in everything.

## The doorstroomtoets and the school advice: what happens when

The dates below are **projected** from the published 2026–27 calendar ([VO-raad dates 2026–27](https://www.vo-raad.nl/nieuws/data-2026-2027-rondom-doorstroomtoets-en-aanmelding-in-het-vo); [Cito information for parents](https://cito.nl/onderwijs/primair-onderwijs/informatie-voor-ouders/over-de-doorstroomtoets/)). The exact 2028–29 dates will be published nearer the time. A single national test may replace the current choice of five tests around 2029–30, so check in groep 7 which test applies.

| When (approx.) | What happens |
|---|---|
| Autumn 2028 | Parent–teacher talks. The school starts forming its view, based on years of work and tracking tests, not one test. |
| By about the end of January 2029 | The school gives the **provisional advice** (*voorlopig schooladvies*): which secondary-school level (vmbo, havo, vwo or a combination) suits him. |
| About late January to mid-February 2029 | **The doorstroomtoets.** Reading (*lezen*), language/spelling (*taalverzorging*) and arithmetic (*rekenen*). Paper or digital, the school chooses. There is a catch-up period for children who are ill. |
| By about mid-March 2029 | Results reach the school. The result gives a **test advice** (*toetsadvies*). |
| About late March 2029 | The **definitive advice** (*definitief schooladvies*). |
| Spring 2029 | Registration at a secondary school (*aanmelding*). |
| September 2029 | Brugklas starts. |

**The most important fact for an anxious child:** the test can only **raise** the advice, never lower it. If the test advice is higher than the provisional advice, the school must reconsider. If it is lower, the provisional advice stands. Unit [8.6](8.6-doorstroomtoets-calm/) explains how to say this to him.

## What the year should feel like

- **Short sessions continue.** 15–20 minutes, 4–5 days a week, stopping while it is going well. See the [parent guide](../PARENT_GUIDE.md).
- **Games keep fluency ticking.** Tables and quick facts are kept warm through [GAMES.md](../GAMES.md), not drills.
- **Test practice comes late and gently.** No test-style practice before mid-December 2028, and never with a timer. Cito's own sample test is used once or twice only (see 8.6).
- **Talk about the brugklas early and often.** By spring he should know roughly what a maths lesson there looks like and how to ask for help (see 8.7).
- **Use the [progress tracker](../PROGRESS.md).** Seeing a growing list of "things I can do now" is one of the best answers to "I'm bad at maths".

## If something isn't working

- If a unit feels too hard, go back one step: the matching groep 7 unit in [../groep-7/](../groep-7/) covers the foundations of 8.1, 8.2 and 8.3.
- If he is upset more than he is calm, pause the worksheets for a week and only play games. The worksheets will still be here.
- If worry about maths affects him at school, ask the teacher whether the school does a "maths conversation" (*rekengesprek*). Cito's tracking test offers one that includes how a child feels about maths (*rekenbeleving*); parents can ask for it ([Cito rekenen-wiskunde](https://cito.nl/onderwijs/primair-onderwijs/leerling-in-beeld-leerlingvolgsysteem/dit-volg-je/rekenen-wiskunde/)).
