# 8.5 Answers: estimating and the calculator

[← Unit overview](README.md) · [Practice](practice.md)

For the parent: in Set B, any sensible estimate made with friendly numbers is correct. The answers below show one good estimate and the exact answer, so you can see how close it was.

## Set A: Rounding

1. **50.** 47 is nearer to 50 than to 40.
2. **400.** Tens digit 8 → round up.
3. **2 000.** Hundreds digit 6 → round up.
4. **4.** 3,7 is nearer to 4.
5. **12.** The tenths digit is 4 → round down. (Don't round twice: 12,49 → 12,5 → 13 is a common slip.)
6. **€5.** €4,95 is 5 cents from €5.
7. **2,3.** The hundredths digit is 4 → round down.
8. **7,9.** Exactly halfway between 7,8 and 7,9 → round up.
9. **16 000.** Exactly halfway → round up.
10. **0,67.** Thousandths digit 6 → round up.
11. **60.** 63 is nearer to 60.

## Set C: Does it make sense?

1. **Right.** 40 × 20 = 800, close to 798.
2. **Wrong.** About 450 + 400 = 850, not 7 000. Correct: 845.
3. **Right.** 1 200 : 4 = 300.
4. **Wrong.** About 3 × €20 = €60. Correct: €59,85 (the comma was in the wrong place).
5. **Wrong.** 50 × 5 = 250, not 2 500. Correct: 50.
6. **Right.** 70 × 50 = 3 500, close to 3 456.
7. **Right.** 9,9 + 0,1 = 10.
8. **Wrong.** 15% is less than the whole €80, so it can't be €120. Correct: €12.
9. **Wrong.** 2 cm is less than a finger is wide. A door is about 2 **m** high.
10. **Right.** 28 : 4 = 7.

## Set E: Reading the calculator

1. **32 minibuses.** 31 are full and the last 2 children still need a seat, so round **up**.
2. **€11,90 each.** 11.9 on the screen means €11,90.
3. **0.3333333 → 0,33.**
4. **About €6,67 each.** 20 : 3 = 6,666… → €6,67. But 3 × €6,67 = €20,01, one cent too much, so one friend pays €6,66. Money doesn't always share out exactly.
5. **A basic calculator may show 14; the correct answer is 11.** Multiply first: 4 × 2 = 8, then 3 + 8 = 11. Many phone and all scientific calculators show 11.
6. **€9,95.** 2,5 × €3,98 = €9,95. Estimate: 2,5 × €4 = €10.
7. **52 full weeks.** The calculator shows 52.142857…; the part after the point is less than a full week (1 day).
8. **13 boxes needed; 12 completely full.** 100 : 8 = 12.5; 12 full boxes hold 96 eggs, the 13th box holds the last 4.
9. **5.** Nice and simple.

## Puzzle corner

1. **Less, by 199.** 100 × 99 = 9 900; take away one more 99 → 9 801. 10 000 − 9 801 = 199.
2. **It depends on the pulse.** Example: 20 beats in 15 seconds → 80 per minute → 80 × 60 × 24 = 115 200 beats a day. Anything from about 100 000 to 140 000 is a sensible answer for a resting child.
3. **111 111 111, 222 222 222, 333 333 333.** 12 345 679 × 9 = 111 111 111, so multiplying by 18 (2 × 9) gives 2 × that, and so on. (Notice the 8 is missing from 12345679.)
