# D2 Integral calculus: problems

[← Module overview](README.md) · [Notes](notes.md) · [Solutions](solutions.md)

Three sections: Practice (problems 1–12), CE-level (*centraal-examenniveau*, problems 13–22) and Challenge (problems 23–28). "Exact" means: no decimals and no numerical integration on the graphing calculator (GR, *grafische rekenmachine*). Where a question gives points, such as (4p), aim for about 1.5 minutes per point, as in the central exam (CE, *centraal examen*).

## Practice

**Problem 1.** Find an antiderivative of each function.

a. $f(x) = 6x^2 - 4x + 3$
b. $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{x^3}$
c. $f(x) = \sqrt{x}$
d. $f(x) = \dfrac{3}{x}$
e. $f(x) = e^{2x+1}$
f. $f(x) = 2^x$
g. $f(x) = \sin(3x)$
h. $f(x) = \cos\left(\tfrac12x - 1\right)$

**Problem 2.** Use the linear substitution rule to find an antiderivative.

a. $f(x) = (2x + 5)^4$
b. $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{3x - 2}$ (for $x > \tfrac23$)
c. $f(x) = e^{-x/2}$
d. $f(x) = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{4x + 1}}$

**Problem 3.** Toon aan dat $F(x) = x\ln x - x$ een primitieve is van $f(x) = \ln x$. Then find the antiderivative $G$ of $\ln x$ with $G(1) = 0$.

**Problem 4.** Let $f(x) = x$ on $[0, 2]$.

a. Compute the lower sum and the upper sum with $n = 4$ equal pieces.
b. Find formulas for the lower sum $L_n$ and the upper sum $U_n$ with $n$ equal pieces. (Use $1 + 2 + \dots + m = \tfrac12m(m+1)$.)
c. Show that both tend to $2$, and explain why this agrees with the area of a triangle.

**Problem 5.** Bereken exact.

a. $\displaystyle\int_1^4\sqrt{x}\,dx$
b. $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/2}\cos x\,dx$
c. $\displaystyle\int_1^e\frac1x\,dx$
d. $\displaystyle\int_0^1 e^{2x}\,dx$

**Problem 6.** a. Bereken exact $\displaystyle\int_0^{2\pi}\sin x\,dx$.
b. Bereken exact the area of the region between the graph of $y = \sin x$ and the $x$-axis on $[0, 2\pi]$.
c. Explain in one sentence why the answers to a and b differ.

**Problem 7.** Gegeven zijn $f(x) = x^2$ en $g(x) = x + 2$. Bereken exact de oppervlakte van het vlakdeel ingesloten door de grafieken van $f$ en $g$.

**Problem 8.** The region under the graph of $f(x) = \sqrt{x}$ on $[0, 4]$ is rotated around the $x$-axis. Bereken exact de inhoud of the solid. What well-known solid is this (its shape, not its name)?

**Problem 9.** Use the reverse chain rule. Check each answer by differentiating.

a. $\displaystyle\int 2x\,e^{x^2}\,dx$
b. $\displaystyle\int \sin x\cos^2x\,dx$
c. $\displaystyle\int \frac{2x}{x^2+1}\,dx$
d. $\displaystyle\int x\,(x^2+3)^4\,dx$

**Problem 10.** Compute the average value of

a. $f(x) = x^2$ on $[0, 3]$;
b. $g(x) = \sin x$ on $[0, \pi]$. Give the exact value.

**Problem 11.** A particle moves along a line with velocity $v(t) = 3t^2 - 12t + 9$ (metres per second) for $0 \le t \le 4$.

a. Compute the displacement between $t = 0$ and $t = 4$.
b. Compute the total distance travelled between $t = 0$ and $t = 4$.

**Problem 12.** Bereken exact de lengte van de grafiek van $f(x) = \tfrac23x^{3/2}$ op $[0, 3]$.

## CE-level

**Problem 13.** Gegeven is de functie $f(x) = x\,e^{-x}$.

a. (3p) Toon aan dat $F(x) = -(x+1)e^{-x}$ een primitieve is van $f$.
b. (3p) Bereken exact de oppervlakte van het vlakdeel ingesloten door de grafiek van $f$, de $x$-as en de lijn $x = 2$.
c. (3p) $W_p$ is the region enclosed by the graph of $f$, the $x$-axis and the line $x = p$, with $p > 0$. Show that the area of $W_p$ is less than $1$ for every $p$, and that it approaches $1$ as $p \to \infty$. (You may use that $p\,e^{-p} \to 0$ as $p \to \infty$.)

**Problem 14.** Gegeven is $f(x) = 4 - x^2$. Het vlakdeel $V$ wordt ingesloten door de grafiek van $f$ en de $x$-as.

a. (3p) Bereken exact de oppervlakte van $V$.
b. (5p) The horizontal line $y = a$ divides $V$ into two parts of equal area. Bereken exact $a$.
c. (4p) Bereken exact de inhoud van het lichaam dat ontstaat als $V$ wentelt om de $x$-as.
d. (4p) Bereken exact de inhoud van het lichaam dat ontstaat als $V$ wentelt om de $y$-as.

**Problem 15.** Gegeven is $f(x) = \ln x$.

a. (3p) Bereken exact de oppervlakte van het vlakdeel ingesloten door de grafiek van $f$, de $x$-as en de lijn $x = e$. (Use problem 3.)
b. (4p) The region bounded by the graph of $f$, the $y$-axis, the $x$-axis and the line $y = 1$ is rotated around the $y$-axis. Bereken exact de inhoud.
c. (3p) Toon aan dat $G(x) = x(\ln x)^2 - 2x\ln x + 2x$ een primitieve is van $g(x) = (\ln x)^2$.
d. (3p) The region from part a is rotated around the $x$-axis. Bereken exact de inhoud.

**Problem 16.** Gegeven zijn $f(x) = \sin 2x$ en $g(x) = \sin x$ op het domein $[0, \pi]$.

a. (4p) Bereken exact de $x$-coördinaten van de snijpunten van de grafieken van $f$ en $g$.
b. (6p) Bereken exact de totale oppervlakte van de vlakdelen ingesloten door de grafieken van $f$ en $g$.

**Problem 17.** a. (4p) The region under the graph of $f(x) = \cos x$ on $[0, \tfrac12\pi]$ is rotated around the $x$-axis. Bereken exact de inhoud. (Use $\cos^2x = \tfrac12(1 + \cos 2x)$.)
b. (4p) The region between the graphs of $y = \cos x$ and $y = \sin x$ on $[0, \tfrac14\pi]$ is rotated around the $x$-axis. Bereken exact de inhoud.

**Problem 18.** Water flows into a tank. The rate of flow in litres per minute is $q(t) = 20t\,e^{-0.1t^2}$, with $t$ in minutes and $t \ge 0$. At $t = 0$ the tank is empty.

a. (3p) Toon aan dat $Q(t) = -100\,e^{-0.1t^2}$ een primitieve is van $q$.
b. (3p) Calculate how many litres flow in during the first 5 minutes. Give the exact value and the value rounded to one decimal.
c. (2p) Calculate the average rate of flow in the first 5 minutes, rounded to one decimal.
d. (3p) The tank holds 120 litres. Show that it never overflows.

**Problem 19.** Gegeven is $f(x) = \tfrac12\left(e^x + e^{-x}\right)$. (This curve is called a catenary, *kettinglijn*: the shape of a hanging chain.)

a. (3p) Toon aan dat $1 + \big(f'(x)\big)^2 = \big(f(x)\big)^2$.
b. (4p) Bereken exact de lengte van de grafiek van $f$ op $[-1, 1]$.
c. (2p) Compare with the area under the graph on $[-1, 1]$. What do you notice?
d. (Extra) Show that for this $f$, the arc length from $0$ to $x$ always equals $f'(x)$.

**Problem 20.** a. (3p) Write down the integral for the length of the graph of $f(x) = x^2$ on $[0, 1]$, and calculate it with the GR, rounded to three decimals.
b. (2p) Without calculating: why must the answer lie between $\sqrt2 \approx 1.414$ and $2$?

**Problem 21.** For every $p > 0$ the function $f_p(x) = px - x^2$ is given.

a. (4p) Show that the area enclosed by the graph of $f_p$ and the $x$-axis equals $\tfrac16p^3$.
b. (2p) Bereken exact voor welke $p$ deze oppervlakte $36$ is.

**Problem 22.** a. (3p) Toon aan dat $F(x) = -\ln(\cos x)$ een primitieve is van $f(x) = \tan x$ op $\left(-\tfrac12\pi, \tfrac12\pi\right)$.
b. (2p) Bereken exact $\displaystyle\int_0^{\pi/4}\tan x\,dx$, and write the answer in the form $a\ln 2$.

## Challenge

**Problem 23.** Use Riemann sums to compute $\displaystyle\int_0^1x^3\,dx$ from the definition. You may use (or, better, prove by induction) that $1^3 + 2^3 + \dots + n^3 = \left(\tfrac12n(n+1)\right)^2$.

**Problem 24.** (Integration by parts, notes §11.)

a. Show that $\int x\,e^x\,dx = (x - 1)e^x + c$ and compute $\int_0^1 x\,e^x\,dx$.
b. Bereken exact $\displaystyle\int_0^\pi x\sin x\,dx$.
c. Use integration by parts twice to find $\int x^2e^x\,dx$, and compare with worked example 2 in the notes.

**Problem 25.** (Improper integrals, notes §11.)

a. Compute $\displaystyle\int_1^\infty\frac{1}{x^2}\,dx$.
b. Gabriel's horn: the region under $y = \frac1x$ for $x \ge 1$ is rotated around the $x$-axis. Show that the volume is finite and compute it.
c. The surface area of a solid of revolution is $2\pi\int f(x)\sqrt{1 + f'(x)^2}\,dx$. Show that for Gabriel's horn this is infinite. (Hint: $\sqrt{1 + f'^2} \ge 1$.) So you can fill the horn with paint but you cannot paint its inside. Explain why this is not a real paradox.

**Problem 26.** a. Prove that if $f$ is continuous and odd ($f(-x) = -f(x)$), then $\int_{-a}^af(x)\,dx = 0$; and if $f$ is even ($f(-x) = f(x)$), then $\int_{-a}^af(x)\,dx = 2\int_0^af(x)\,dx$. Use an antiderivative argument or the Riemann-sum picture.
b. Bereken exact $\displaystyle\int_{-1}^1\left(x^3\cos x + x^2\right)dx$ (no antiderivative of $x^3\cos x$ needed).

**Problem 27.** a. Rotate the upper half of the circle $x^2 + y^2 = r^2$ around the $x$-axis and derive the volume of a sphere.
b. Derive the volume of a spherical cap (*bolsegment*) of height $h$ cut from a sphere of radius $r$ ($0 \le h \le 2r$). Check your formula for $h = r$ and $h = 2r$.

**Problem 28.** Suppose you only know the definition $L(x) = \displaystyle\int_1^x\frac1t\,dt$ for $x > 0$ (forget everything about $\ln$).

a. Explain why $L'(x) = \frac1x$ and $L(1) = 0$.
b. Fix $a > 0$ and let $h(x) = L(ax) - L(x)$. Show that $h'(x) = 0$, and conclude that $L(ax) = L(a) + L(x)$.
c. Compute $\dfrac{d}{dx}\displaystyle\int_0^{x^2}e^{t^2}\,dt$.
