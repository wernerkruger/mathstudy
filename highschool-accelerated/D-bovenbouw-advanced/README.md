# Stage D: upper-school advanced (klas 3, 2029–30)

[← Back to the track overview](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) · [Progress tracker](../PROGRESS.md)

**When:** klas 3 of gymnasium, school year 2029–30 (he is about 15).
**Length:** 38 study weeks of about 4 hours. There are no built-in buffer weeks, so use school holidays for catch-up, or let D4 run into the summer if needed.
**Previous stage:** [C: upper-school core](../C-bovenbouw-core/) · **Next stage:** [E: Wiskunde D and exams](../E-wiskunde-d-and-exams/)

## What this stage does

Stage C built the function toolkit and the first half of calculus. Stage D completes the calculus and geometry core of the central exam (*centraal examen*, CE) and adds probability, which is new in the CE under wiskunde techniek:

- **D1** finishes differentiation: the chain rule (*kettingregel*), the derivatives of all standard functions, implicit differentiation (*impliciet differentiëren*), related rates, full curve sketching (*functieonderzoek*) and functions with a parameter.
- **D2** introduces integration: antiderivatives (*primitieven*), Riemann sums, the fundamental theorem (*hoofdstelling van de integraalrekening*), areas, volumes of revolution (*omwentelingslichamen*) and arc length (*booglengte*).
- **D3** is coordinate geometry with vectors: lines, distances, circles, the dot product (*inproduct*), and parametric curves (*parameterkrommen*) with velocity and acceleration vectors.
- **D4** is probability and statistics: random variables (*toevalsvariabelen*), expected value and standard deviation, the binomial and normal distributions, hypothesis tests (*hypothesetoetsen*), correlation and regression.

After this stage he has covered the whole Wiskunde B CE syllabus (domains B–E of the CvTE syllabus 2028) and the probability part of wiskunde techniek. Stage E then adds the rest of wiskunde techniek and Wiskunde D (differential equations, complex numbers, geometric proof and conic sections) and exam practice.

Klas 3 is the year his school's own programme still covers lower-school topics (*onderbouw*: factorising, the abc-formula, first trigonometry). Agree with the school that he may compact these lessons and use the time for this stage. The [parent guide](../PARENT_GUIDE.md) explains how.

## Order and dependencies

```
C3 ──> D1 ──> D2 ──> D3 ──> D4
              │       ^
              └───────┘  (arc length of parametric curves uses D2)
```

- D1 must come first: everything in D2 and the motion part of D3 depends on the chain rule.
- D2 before D3 is preferred, because D3 uses integrals for the arc length of parametric curves (a "going further" topic).
- D4 depends only on stage B probability ([B5](../B-onderbouw-compact/B5-data-probability-counting/)) and a little of D2 (the normal distribution as an area under a curve). If calculus starts to feel heavy, D4 can be moved earlier or run alongside D3 at one hour a week.

## How to work in this stage

- **Exam style.** By now most problems should be done in CE style: "bereken exact" (calculate exactly, no calculator), "toon aan" (show that: a complete derivation of a given result), and "bereken" (a graphing calculator, *grafische rekenmachine* or GR, is allowed). Write the reasoning, not just the answer; CE marking gives points for steps.
- **Past exams.** From D1 on, do a few real CE questions each week from past VWO Wiskunde B exams with their marking schemes (*correctievoorschrift*) at [alleexamens.nl](https://www.alleexamens.nl/examens/VWO/Wiskunde-B/). Each module README says which topics to look for. Mark them strictly against the marking scheme.
- **Proofs.** Each module proves its main rules (the chain rule, the fundamental theorem, the equivalence of the two dot-product definitions, $E(X+Y)=E(X)+E(Y)$). He should be able to reproduce these proofs on a blank sheet.
- **Challenge problems.** These are optional. If one takes more than an hour, stop and come back to it a week later.

## Exit criteria for the stage

He is ready for stage E when he can:

1. Pass each module's exit criteria (see the module READMEs).
2. Score at least 70% on a complete past VWO Wiskunde B CE, done under exam conditions (3 hours, GR allowed) and marked with the official marking scheme. I'm assuming 70% is a sensible bar for a pupil aiming at the highest grades.
3. Reproduce, without notes, the proofs of the chain rule, the derivative of $\sin x$, the fundamental theorem (sketch), the distance formula from a point to a line, and the expected value and variance of the binomial distribution.

Record progress in the [progress tracker](../PROGRESS.md).
