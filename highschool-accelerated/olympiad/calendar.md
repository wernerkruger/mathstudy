# Competition calendar (wedstrijdkalender)

> **Check the dates every year.** The dates below are the published 2026–27 dates where we have them, and the usual month otherwise. Each September, look up the new dates on wiskundeolympiade.nl, w4kangoeroe.nl, the VU page for the JWO and pyth.eu, and make sure the school has registered. Months marked "usually" have no confirmed date here.

| Month | What happens | What he does |
|---|---|---|
| **September** | Schools can register for the NWO first round (from early September; 2026 deadline 7 January 2027). NWO final at TU Eindhoven for last year's qualifiers. A new Pythagoras volume starts; I'm assuming its first Olympiade problems appear around now. | Ask the maths teacher to register the school for the NWO and the Kangoeroe. Start the year's module. |
| **October** | Pythagoras Olympiade problems (6 issues a year). | Module work. Send in the easier Pythagoras problems. |
| **November** | Pythagoras Olympiade problems. | Module work. |
| **December** | Pythagoras Olympiade problems. Last weeks to register a school for the NWO. | Sit one past NWO first-round paper under exam conditions (2 hours). |
| **January** | **NWO first round** at school (2027: 18–29 January), klas 1–5. | Second mock first-round paper early in the month, then the real round. |
| **February** | NWO first-round results and invitations to the second round (usually). Pythagoras problems. | If invited: past second-round papers, full written solutions. |
| **March** | **NWO second round** at universities (2027: 19 March). **W4Kangoeroe** at school (2027: 18–26 March). | Second round if invited; Kangoeroe. |
| **April** | Second-round results and invitations to the final and training (usually). Kangoeroe results; the best 200 are invited to the JWO (usually). Pythagoras problems. | Module work. If invited to the JWO: past JWO papers. |
| **May** | **NWO training** for finalists: four afternoons in May–June at several universities. | Training if invited. Lighter module work around school tests. |
| **June** | NWO training continues. **Junior Wiskunde Olympiade** at the VU Amsterdam on a Saturday in late June (2026: 27 June). | JWO if invited. Write up a short review of the year in the problem journal. |
| **July** | IMO and other international competitions for selected team members. Summer holiday. | Rest. Optional: a few Pythagoras or Alcumus problems for fun. |
| **August** | Summer holiday. | If he is a finalist: two or three past NWO final papers in late August. |
