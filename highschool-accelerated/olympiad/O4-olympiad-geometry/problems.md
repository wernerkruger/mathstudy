# O4 Olympiad geometry: problems

Thirty problems in three levels. For every problem, first draw a large accurate diagram (compass and ruler, or GeoGebra). Write down a final answer for every computation and a complete argument for every "prove" problem. Hints are at the end; full solutions are in [solutions.md](solutions.md).

---

## Level 1 — Kangoeroe style (wizPROF)

**1.** In triangle $ABC$, $AB = AC$ and $\angle A = 40^\circ$. The bisector of angle $B$ meets $AC$ at $D$. Find $\angle BDC$.
*Topic: angle chasing*

**2.** In triangle $ABC$, $AB = AC$. Point $D$ lies on side $AC$ such that $AD = BD = BC$. Find $\angle A$.
*Topic: angle chasing, isosceles triangles*

**3.** $ABCD$ is a cyclic quadrilateral (vertices in this order on the circle) whose diagonals meet at $P$. Given $\angle ADB = 40^\circ$ and $\angle DBC = 25^\circ$, find $\angle APB$.
*Topic: inscribed angles*

**4.** Five points $A, B, C, D, E$ lie in this order on a circle (not necessarily equally spaced). Draw the five-pointed star $A \to C \to E \to B \to D \to A$. Find the sum of the five angles at the tips of the star, $\angle CAD + \angle DBE + \angle ECA + \angle ADB + \angle BEC$.
*Topic: angle chasing, exterior angles*

**5.** A right triangle has legs 3 and 4. A square is placed inside it so that one corner of the square is at the right angle, two sides of the square lie along the legs, and the opposite corner lies on the hypotenuse. Find the side of the square.
*Topic: similar triangles*

**6.** In triangle $ABC$, $AB = 6$, $AC = 9$ and $BC = 10$. The bisector of angle $A$ meets $BC$ at $D$. Find $BD$.
*Topic: angle bisector theorem*

**7.** Two chords $AB$ and $CD$ of a circle intersect at $P$. Given $AP = 4$, $PB = 6$ and $CP = 3$, find the length of $CD$.
*Topic: power of a point*

**8.** A circle has centre $O$ and radius 5. Point $P$ satisfies $PO = 13$. A line through $P$ meets the circle at $A$ and $B$, with $A$ between $P$ and $B$, and $PA = 9$. Find $AB$.
*Topic: power of a point, tangents*

**9.** A triangle has sides 13, 14 and 15. Find its area, its inradius $r$ and its circumradius $R$.
*Topic: areas, Heron, $rs$, $abc/4R$*

## Level 2 — NWO first/second round style

**10.** In triangle $ABC$, point $D$ lies on $BC$ with $BD : DC = 1 : 2$, and $E$ is the midpoint of $AD$. Line $BE$ meets $AC$ at $F$. Find $AF : FC$ and $BE : EF$.
*Topic: Menelaus, area ratios*

**11.** In triangle $ABC$, $AB = 5$, $AC = 6$ and $BC = 7$. Find the length of the median from $A$.
*Topic: median length, Stewart*

**12.** In triangle $ABC$, $\angle A = 60^\circ$, $\angle B = 45^\circ$ and $BC = 6$. Find $AC$, $AB$ and the area of the triangle in exact form.
*Topic: sine rule with $2R$, areas*

**13.** Two circles meet at $P$ and $Q$. A common tangent touches the first circle at $T_1$ and the second at $T_2$. Prove that the line $PQ$ passes through the midpoint of $T_1T_2$.
*Topic: power of a point, radical axis*

**14.** In an acute triangle $ABC$, $BE$ and $CF$ are altitudes ($E$ on $AC$, $F$ on $AB$). Prove that $B, C, E, F$ lie on one circle, and that $\angle AEF = \angle ABC$.
*Topic: cyclic quadrilaterals, Thales*

**15.** Triangle $ABC$ has $AB = 13$, $BC = 14$ and $CA = 15$, and incentre $I$. Line $AI$ meets the circumcircle again at $M$. Find $AI$ and $MI$, and conclude that $I$ is the midpoint of $AM$.
*Topic: incentre/excentre lemma, trigonometry*

**16.** $ABCD$ is a parallelogram with area 60. $M$ is the midpoint of $CD$, and $BM$ meets the diagonal $AC$ at $P$. Find the area of quadrilateral $APMD$.
*Topic: similar triangles, area ratios*

**17.** The incircle of triangle $ABC$ touches $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ at $D$, $E$, $F$. Prove that $AD$, $BE$ and $CF$ pass through one point.
*Topic: Ceva, tangent lengths*

**18.** A triangle has sides 5, 7 and 8. Find the angle opposite the side of length 7, the area, the inradius and the circumradius.
*Topic: cosine rule, areas*

**19.** A square has side 2. A circle passes through two adjacent vertices of the square and is tangent to the opposite side. Find its radius.
*Topic: power of a point or Pythagoras*

**20.** In triangle $ABC$, $\angle A = 70^\circ$, $\angle B = 60^\circ$ and $\angle C = 50^\circ$. Let $H$, $O$ and $I$ be the orthocentre, circumcentre and incentre. Find $\angle BHC$, $\angle BOC$ and $\angle BIC$.
*Topic: triangle centres, angle chasing*

**21.** In triangle $ABC$, $AB = 4$, $AC = 6$ and $BC = 5$. Find the length of the bisector from $A$ (from $A$ to its intersection with $BC$).
*Topic: angle bisector theorem, Stewart*

## Level 3 — NWO final / BxMO style

**22.** In triangle $ABC$, $AB = AC$ and $\angle A = 20^\circ$. Point $D$ lies on side $AC$ with $\angle DBC = 60^\circ$, and point $E$ lies on side $AB$ with $\angle ECB = 50^\circ$. Find $\angle BDE$, with a synthetic proof.
*Topic: angle chasing with an auxiliary point*

**23.** Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with orthocentre $H$, circumcentre $O$, and let $M$ be the midpoint of $BC$.
(a) Prove that the reflection of $H$ in line $BC$ lies on the circumcircle.
(b) Prove that the reflection of $H$ in the point $M$ is the point of the circumcircle diametrically opposite $A$.
(c) Deduce that $AH = 2\,OM$.
*Topic: orthocentre, cyclic quadrilaterals*

**24.** Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with altitudes $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ and orthocentre $H$. Prove that $H$ is the incentre of triangle $DEF$.
*Topic: cyclic quadrilaterals, triangle centres*

**25.** (Simson line.) Point $P$ lies on the circumcircle of triangle $ABC$. Let $X$, $Y$, $Z$ be the feet of the perpendiculars from $P$ to the lines $BC$, $CA$, $AB$. Prove that $X$, $Y$, $Z$ are collinear.
*Topic: cyclic quadrilaterals, directed angles*

**26.** (Brahmagupta.) $ABCD$ is a cyclic quadrilateral whose diagonals are perpendicular and meet at $P$. The line through $P$ perpendicular to $AB$ meets $CD$ at $M$. Prove that $M$ is the midpoint of $CD$.
*Topic: angle chasing, inscribed angles*

**27.** Let $ABC$ be a triangle with three different side lengths. The external bisectors of the angles at $A$, $B$, $C$ meet the lines $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ at $X$, $Y$, $Z$. Prove that $X$, $Y$, $Z$ are collinear.
*Topic: Menelaus, angle bisector theorem*

**28.** Triangle $ABC$ has $AB = 13$, $BC = 14$ and $CA = 15$. Find the distance between its circumcentre $O$ and its incentre $I$.
*Topic: triangle centres, coordinates*

**29.** Let $ABC$ be an acute triangle with orthocentre $H$. Points $E$ and $F$ lie on the sides $CA$ and $AB$. Prove that $H$ has the same power with respect to the circle with diameter $BE$ and the circle with diameter $CF$. Conclude that if these two circles meet in two points, the line through those points passes through $H$.
*Topic: power of a point, radical axis*

**30.** In triangle $ABC$, the points $D$, $E$, $F$ lie on $BC$, $CA$, $AB$ with $BD : DC = CE : EA = AF : FB = 1 : 2$. The segments $AD$, $BE$, $CF$ bound a small triangle in the middle. What fraction of the area of $ABC$ is it? Prove your answer.
*Topic: Menelaus, area ratios*

## Hints

1. Find $\angle ABD$ first; $\angle BDC$ is an exterior angle of triangle $ABD$.
2. Call $\angle A = x$ and express every angle in terms of $x$.
3. $\angle ACB = \angle ADB$ and $\angle DAC = \angle DBC$; then use an exterior angle of triangle $BPC$.
4. Let $AC$ and $AD$ meet $BE$ at $P$ and $Q$; look at triangles $APQ$, $PCE$ and $QBD$.
5. The small triangle above the square is similar to the whole triangle.
6. $BD : DC = AB : AC$.
7. $AP \cdot PB = CP \cdot PD$.
8. Compute the tangent length from $P$ first.
9. Heron with $s = 21$; then area $= rs$ and $R = \frac{abc}{4 \cdot \text{area}}$.
10. Menelaus in triangle $ADC$ with the line $BEF$; then in triangle $BFC$ with the line $AED$.
11. $m_a^2 = \frac{2b^2 + 2c^2 - a^2}{4}$.
12. $\angle C = 75^\circ$ and $2R = \frac{6}{\sin 60^\circ}$.
13. Let $PQ$ meet $T_1T_2$ at $N$ and compute the power of $N$ with respect to both circles.
14. Two right angles on the segment $BC$.
15. $\cos A = \frac{33}{65}$; find $\sin\frac A2$; $AI = \frac{r}{\sin(A/2)}$ and $MB = 2R\sin\frac A2$.
16. Triangles $PAB$ and $PCM$ are similar with ratio $2$.
17. Tangent lengths are $s-a$, $s-b$, $s-c$; use the converse of Ceva.
18. The cosine rule gives $\cos = \frac12$.
19. Put the centre on the axis of symmetry of the square and use Pythagoras.
20. $\angle BHC = 180^\circ - A$, $\angle BOC = 2A$, $\angle BIC = 90^\circ + \frac12 A$.
21. First $BD$ and $DC$ from the angle bisector theorem, then Stewart.
22. Take $G$ on $AC$ with $\angle GBC = 20^\circ$. Show that $BEG$ is equilateral and $GD = GB$.
23. (a) $\angle BHC = 180^\circ - A$. (b) $BHCH'$ is a parallelogram; show $\angle ABH' = 90^\circ$.
24. Find the cyclic quadrilaterals with diameters $BH$ and $CH$; show $\angle HDF = \angle HDE = 90^\circ - A$.
25. $PXCY$ and $PZAY$ are cyclic (Thales). Compare $\measuredangle PYX$ and $\measuredangle PYZ$.
26. In right triangle $APB$ with altitude $PF$: $\angle APF = \angle ABP$. Then show triangle $MPC$ is isosceles.
27. The external bisector divides the opposite side externally in the ratio of the adjacent sides.
28. Coordinates $B = (0,0)$, $C = (14, 0)$, $A = (5, 12)$; or Euler's formula $OI^2 = R^2 - 2Rr$.
29. The foot of the altitude from $B$ lies on the circle with diameter $BE$.
30. Show that each cevian is cut in the ratio $3 : 3 : 1$; then the triangle with side $AB$ and third vertex $AD \cap BE$ has area $\frac27 [ABC]$.
