## Stage A: groep 8 foundations

- AoPS *Prealgebra* (the main book for this stage) and the start of *Introduction to Algebra* and *Introduction to Number Theory*.
- Khan Academy: the pre-algebra and arithmetic courses (<https://www.khanacademy.org/>).
- WiskundeAcademie: the klas 1 and klas 2 playlists (<https://www.wiskundeacademie.nl/>).
- W4Kangoeroe past papers (wizSMART and wizBRAIN) in Dutch and English with answers: <https://www.w4kangoeroe.nl/kangoeroe/index.php/historie>. Good challenge material for every Stage A module.
- Hans Magnus Enzensberger, *The Number Devil* (*De telduivel*). A story book about numbers; read for fun.
- Reading: Ian Stewart, *Professor Stewart's Cabinet of Mathematical Curiosities*. Puzzles and short pieces.

## Stage B: klas 1, onderbouw compact

- AoPS *Introduction to Algebra*, *Introduction to Geometry*, *Introduction to Counting & Probability*.
- WiskundeAcademie: the havo/vwo 2 and 3 playlists.
- Khan Academy: algebra 1, geometry, and statistics and probability courses.
- AoPS Alcumus for daily practice at the right level.
- The onderbouw core targets (kerndoelen 10–17) explained by SLO: <https://www.slo.nl/publish/pages/22237/definitieve-conceptkerndoelen-rekenen-wiskunde-toelichtingsdocument.pdf>.

## Stage C: klas 2, upper-school core

- AoPS *Intermediate Algebra* (functions, polynomials, exponentials and logarithms) and *Precalculus* (trigonometry).
- WiskundeAcademie: the vwo Wiskunde B playlists.
- Khan Academy: algebra 2, trigonometry, precalculus, and differential calculus.
- Past VWO Wiskunde B central exams with marking schemes (since 1999): <https://www.alleexamens.nl/examens/VWO/Wiskunde-B/>. Start doing selected questions in this stage. (The official source is examenblad.nl.)
- The Wiskunde B syllabus (concept 2028): <https://static.alleexamens.nl/programma/vwo/2028/Wiskunde%20B.pdf>.

## Stage D: klas 3, upper-school advanced

- AoPS *Calculus*; AoPS *Precalculus* (vectors).
- Khan Academy: integral calculus, statistics and probability.
- Past VWO Wiskunde B central exams (above): full papers under exam conditions.
- The new wiskunde techniek programme (definitive concept, September 2026): <https://www.slo.nl/publish/pages/22945/definitief-conceptexamenprogramma-wiskunde-techniek-vwo.pdf>.

## Stage E: klas 4, Wiskunde D and exam practice

- The current Wiskunde D programme described by DUO: <https://duo.nl/images/wiskunde-d-(vwo-2026).pdf>.
- The renewal of Wiskunde D (final programmes September 2029): <https://www.actualisatie-examenprogrammas.nl/wiskunde-havo-vwo/wiskunde-d>.
- SLO overview of all renewed havo/vwo maths programmes: <https://www.actualisatie-examenprogrammas.nl/wiskunde-havo-vwo>.
- AoPS *Precalculus* (complex numbers); AoPS *Introduction to Geometry* (conics, circle theorems).
- Past VWO Wiskunde B central exams: all recent years, timed.

## Stage F: klas 5–6, beyond school

Pre-university programmes (check entry rules each year; most need a partner school):

- Leiden University PRE-College (for 5 and 6 vwo at partner schools): <https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/pre-college/about-pre-college>. Leiden Pre-Classes for bovenbouw vwo: <https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/scholen/scholieren-en-wetenschap/extra-uitdaging-voor-leerlingen>.
- Radboud Pre-University College (bovenbouw vwo; up to two first-year university courses per semester): <https://www.ru.nl/opleidingen/meer-onderwijs-en-trainingen/programmas-voor-leerlingen/extra-uitdaging>. Radboud Pre-University College of Science: <https://www.ru.nl/en/departments/institute-for-science-education/radboud-pre-university-college-of-science>.

University-level material in this repository:

- The adult track [`../adult-masters/`](../adult-masters/), especially [1.1 Proofs, logic and sets](../adult-masters/01-refresher/1.1-proofs-logic-sets/), [1.2 Real analysis I](../adult-masters/01-refresher/1.2-real-analysis-I/) and [1.3 Linear algebra](../adult-masters/01-refresher/1.3-linear-algebra/).
