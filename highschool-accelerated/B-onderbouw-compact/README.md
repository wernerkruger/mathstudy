# Stage B: onderbouw compact (klas 1)

[← Back to the track overview](../README.md)

**When:** klas 1 (*brugklas*) of gymnasium, school year 2027–28 (age 13)
**Length:** 38 weeks at about 4 hours a week (about 150 hours), September 2027 to early July 2028
**Previous stage:** [A: groep 8 foundations](../A-groep8-foundations/)
**Next stage:** [C: bovenbouw core](../C-bovenbouw-core/)

## What this stage is for

Dutch VWO spreads the lower-school (*onderbouw*) mathematics over three years, klas 1 to 3. Stage B compresses all of it into **one year**. By the end of klas 1 he will have covered everything a strong VWO pupil knows at the end of klas 3: linear and quadratic relations, the abc-formula (*abc-formule*), Pythagoras, similarity and trigonometry in right triangles, powers and exponential growth, statistics, probability and counting.

The pace is fast, but the depth is not sacrificed. As in stage A, every rule comes with a reason, and the main results come with proofs: the abc-formula is derived, not just used; Pythagoras gets several proofs and its converse; the trigonometric ratios are justified by similarity; the formula for $\binom{n}{k}$ is explained by counting. Stage B is also where **proof** (*bewijzen*) becomes a skill in its own right, in module B3.

By the end of the stage he should:

- manipulate algebraic expressions quickly and accurately, including factorising (*ontbinden in factoren*);
- solve linear, quadratic and simple rational equations, inequalities and systems of two equations, and know why each method is valid;
- move freely between formula, table and graph for linear, quadratic, exponential and power relations;
- use Pythagoras, similarity and sine, cosine and tangent in flat figures and in 3D solids;
- write a clear proof using congruent triangles, angle theorems or algebra;
- summarise and compare data, calculate probabilities with rules and tree diagrams, and count arrangements and selections.

### Order and dependencies

- **B1 before B2.** Quadratics use linear equations, and intersecting a parabola with a line uses both.
- **B2 before B4.** Simplifying roots and powers uses the algebra of B2.
- **B3 can start in the last weeks of B2.** It needs square roots and solving equations, but no quadratics beyond $x^2 = c$. Running B2 and B3 side by side for a few weeks gives variety.
- **B5 is largely independent.** It can be moved earlier, for example to line up with the statistics chapter at school. The counting sections are also good preparation for the Kangoeroe (see below).

## How stage B maps to the curriculum

The lower-school curriculum is set by the national core objectives (*kerndoelen*). New kerndoelen for rekenen-wiskunde became law in August 2026, and all schools must follow them by August 2031. His school may still be teaching to the old kerndoelen in 2027–28; the content for VWO is very similar.

| Kerndoel (new numbering) | Covered in |
|---|---|
| 10 Numbers, quantities and equations (*getallen, grootheden en vergelijkingen*): algebra, linear, quadratic and power equations, systems, inequalities | B1, B2, B4 |
| 11 Data and probability (*data en kansen*): bivariate data, levels of measurement, IQR, correlation and causation, probability rules, combinatorics | B5 |
| 12 Patterns and relations (*patronen en verbanden*): function concept, standard relations, how quantities change | B1, B2, B4 |
| 13 Geometry (*meetkunde*): 2D and 3D, sight lines; VWO addition: reasoning with angles, trigonometric ratios in right triangles | B3 |
| 14 Ways of thinking and working (*denk- en werkwijzen*): heuristics, modelling, proof | all, especially B3 |
| 15–17 Mathematical language and tools, attitude, applying mathematics | all |

Looking ahead, stage B is the foundation for every domain of the upper-school exam: functions, graphs and equations (Wiskunde B domain B, wiskunde techniek A4 *verbanden*), geometry (Wiskunde B domain E, wiskunde techniek A6 *meetkunde* and B3 *redeneren en bewijzen*), and probability (wiskunde techniek A3 *data en kans*, Wiskunde D domain B). Source: SLO, *definitieve conceptkerndoelen rekenen-wiskunde* (https://www.slo.nl/publish/pages/22237/definitieve-conceptkerndoelen-rekenen-wiskunde-toelichtingsdocument.pdf).

## Aligning with school

Stage B only works if the school knows about it. Without an agreement he would do 4 hours of mathematics at home on top of 3–4 lessons a week of material he has already mastered, which leads to boredom in class and too much total work. The [parent guide](../PARENT_GUIDE.md) covers the conversation with the school in more detail; the essentials for klas 1 are below.

### Ask for compacting

*Compacten en verrijken* (compacting and enrichment) is a standard practice in Dutch secondary schools for gifted pupils. The request to the mathematics teacher and the mentor or *begaafdheidscoördinator*, ideally before the autumn break:

1. **Test out instead of sitting through.** He takes each chapter test (*hoofdstuktoets*) or a pre-test at the start of the chapter. If he scores well, he skips the routine exercises of that chapter in class.
2. **Use the freed lesson time for this track.** He works on the current stage B module during maths lessons, so most of the 4 hours a week happens at school rather than on top of it.
3. **Keep the school record intact.** He still sits the regular tests (*toetsen*) and *toetsweek* exams, so his report (*rapport*) stays complete. These will cost him little preparation.
4. **Agree a contact point.** A teacher who looks at his work every few weeks, even briefly, makes a big difference. Show them the module's "I can…" checklist and exit criteria.
5. **Ask about a plusklas or enrichment programme**, and about entering competitions through the school (below).

Bring evidence: completed stage A work, and a sample of his written proofs. A short written plan (this README and the module list) helps the teacher say yes.

### What school will be teaching meanwhile

The order below is typical for Getal & Ruimte and Moderne Wiskunde VWO, but methods and schools differ, so check against his own book.

| School year | Typical school topics | Where it is covered here |
|---|---|---|
| Klas 1 (2027–28, his actual year) | Negative numbers, plane figures and symmetry, graphs and formulas, calculating with letters, area and volume, angles, solids and views, statistics | Stage A, plus B1 (formulas, graphs), B3 (angles, solids, views), B5 (statistics) |
| Klas 2 (normally) | Linear formulas, equations, brackets, Pythagoras, quadratic formulas and parabolas, similarity, powers, roots, statistics, probability | B1, B2, B3, B4, B5 |
| Klas 3 (normally) | Factorising, abc-formula, systems of equations, sin/cos/tan, exponential growth, power functions, rational equations, transformations, combinatorics, difference quotient | B1, B2, B3, B4, B5; transformations and the difference quotient come in stage C |

So **the klas 1 lessons are almost entirely review** for him: stage A already covered that material. That is the strongest argument for compacting. In klas 2 and 3, when he is in stages C and D, everything school teaches will already be familiar.

Two practical points:

- **Notation and conventions.** Schools use specific conventions (for example, the Dutch quartile convention for boxplots, and the order of steps expected in an answer). The modules point these out; where the school's version differs, he should use the school's in school tests.
- **Graphing calculator.** Dutch VWO pupils normally get a graphing calculator (often a TI-84) in klas 3 or 4. He does not need one in stage B; all problems can be done by hand or with a simple scientific calculator. A free graphing tool such as GeoGebra or Desmos is useful for checking graphs.

## Competitions in klas 1

The [olympiad track](../olympiad/) runs alongside at about 1 hour a week. Klas 1 is the first year he can enter the main Dutch competitions:

- **Nederlandse Wiskunde Olympiade, first round**: at school in the second half of January (2028 dates to be confirmed). The school must register. Stage B and the olympiad modules O1 (problem solving) and O2 (number theory) are good preparation.
- **W4Kangoeroe**: in March, through school. In klas 1 his category is wizBRAIN (klas 1–2 havo/vwo); he may sit the harder wizPROF instead. Past problems: https://www.w4kangoeroe.nl/kangoeroe/index.php/historie
- **Junior Wiskunde Olympiade** (VU Amsterdam): invitation for klas 1–2 pupils with top Kangoeroe scores.
- **Pythagoras Olympiade**: problems throughout the year; archive at https://www.pyth.eu/olympiade

In the weeks before a competition, swap one week of the module plan for past papers. The calendar has room for about two such weeks.

## Exit criteria for the stage

He is ready for [stage C](../C-bovenbouw-core/) when he has met the exit criteria of all five modules, and in particular when he can, without notes:

- solve any quadratic equation by the most efficient method, and derive the abc-formula;
- find the vertex of a parabola and the intersection of a parabola and a line;
- solve a right-triangle or 3D problem with Pythagoras and trigonometry, giving exact values where possible;
- write a correct proof with congruent triangles;
- work with negative and fractional exponents and set up an exponential formula from a context;
- calculate a probability with a tree diagram or $\binom{n}{k}$.

If two or more modules are not secure by July 2028, it is better to spend the first weeks of klas 2 finishing stage B than to start stage C on a weak foundation.
