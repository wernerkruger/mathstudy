## Objectives

By the end of this module you can:

1. Expand brackets (*haakjes wegwerken*) using the distributive law (*distributieve eigenschap*), including the special products (*merkwaardige producten*) $(a+b)^2$, $(a-b)^2$ and $(a+b)(a-b)$, and explain each one with an area picture.
2. Factorise (*ontbinden in factoren*): take out a common factor (*buiten haakjes brengen*), use the product-sum method for $x^2+bx+c$, recognise a difference of squares, and factorise $ax^2+bx+c$.
3. Solve quadratic equations (*kwadratische vergelijkingen*) with the product-is-zero rule (*product-is-nul*), by taking square roots for $x^2=c$, by completing the square (*kwadraat afsplitsen*) and with the abc-formula (*abc-formule*), and explain why each method works.
4. Derive the abc-formula from completing the square, and use the discriminant (*discriminant*) to decide how many solutions an equation has.
5. Sketch and analyse a parabola (*parabool*) $y=ax^2+bx+c$: opening up or down (*dalparabool/bergparabool*), vertex (*top*), axis of symmetry (*symmetrieas*), and intersections with the axes and with lines.
6. Solve quadratic inequalities (*kwadratische ongelijkheden*) using a sketch.
7. Solve simple rational equations (*gebroken vergelijkingen*) and reject extraneous roots where a denominator becomes zero.
8. Model and solve optimisation problems such as the largest area for a fixed perimeter.

## I can…

- [ ] expand products such as $(2x-3)(x+4)$ quickly and without mistakes
- [ ] state the three special products and draw the area picture for each
- [ ] explain why $(a+b)^2 \neq a^2+b^2$ (unless $a=0$ or $b=0$)
- [ ] take out a common factor (*buiten haakjes brengen*)
- [ ] factorise $x^2+bx+c$ with the product-sum method
- [ ] factorise a difference of squares, also when a common factor comes first
- [ ] factorise $ax^2+bx+c$ by splitting the middle term
- [ ] solve an equation with the product-is-zero rule and explain why it works for $0$ but not for $6$
- [ ] solve $x^2=c$ and know what happens when $c<0$
- [ ] complete the square (*kwadraat afsplitsen*)
- [ ] derive the abc-formula from scratch
- [ ] use the discriminant to give the number of solutions
- [ ] find the vertex (*top*) and axis of symmetry (*symmetrieas*) of a parabola in two ways
- [ ] find where a parabola meets the axes and a given line
- [ ] solve a quadratic inequality with a sketch
- [ ] solve a rational equation (*gebroken vergelijking*) and check for zero denominators
- [ ] set up and solve an optimisation problem with a quadratic formula
- [ ] use Vieta's formulas for the sum and product of the roots (going further)

## Exit criteria

You are ready for the next module when:

1. You have solved all Practice problems (1–13) and checked them against the solutions, with at most a few slips that you can find and correct yourself.
2. You can solve at least 7 of the 9 School-exam level problems (14–22) without help, in a time similar to a school test (*toets*): about 50 minutes for any 4 of them.
3. You have made a serious attempt at every Challenge problem (23–30) and fully solved at least 4.
4. You can derive the abc-formula on a blank sheet of paper, explaining each step out loud.
5. You can explain, without notes, why the vertex of $y=ax^2+bx+c$ lies at $x=-\frac{b}{2a}$ and why a rational equation can produce a "solution" that must be rejected.
