# A2 Problems: Fractions, ratios, percentages and powers

[← Module overview](README.md) · [Notes](notes.md) · [Solutions](solutions.md)

No calculator for problems 1–8 and for the challenge problems, unless stated. A calculator is allowed for problems 9–17, but write down the calculation you type in. Give fractions in lowest terms.

---

## Practice

**1.** Calculate.
(a) $\dfrac23 + \dfrac14$  (b) $\dfrac56 - \dfrac38$  (c) $2\tfrac13 - 1\tfrac34$  (d) $\dfrac79 \div \dfrac{14}{15}$  (e) $\dfrac34 \div \dfrac25$  (f) $\dfrac12 + \dfrac13 + \dfrac16$

**2.** (a) Write $0.375$ and $0.45$ as fractions.
(b) Write $\dfrac58$ and $\dfrac17$ as decimals.
(c) Write $0.\overline{27}$ and $0.1\overline{6}$ as fractions.

**3.** Without dividing, decide which of these give a terminating decimal. Explain each answer.

$$
\frac{3}{40},\qquad \frac{7}{12},\qquad \frac{9}{75},\qquad \frac{11}{56},\qquad \frac{13}{125}
$$

**4.** (a) Share €360 between three people in the ratio $2 : 3 : 4$.
(b) A recipe uses 300 g of pasta for 4 people. Use a ratio table (*verhoudingstabel*) to find how much pasta you need for 7 people.
(c) Concrete is mixed from cement, sand and gravel in the ratio $1 : 2 : 4$. How many kg of each are in 35 kg of concrete?

**5.** (a) What is 15% of 80?
(b) After a 20% discount a jacket costs €64. What was the original price?
(c) A price rises from €50 to €58. By what percentage?
(d) A price falls from €120 to €90. By what percentage?

**6.** Calculate, without a calculator.
(a) $2^{10}$  (b) $(-3)^3$  (c) $(-3)^4$  (d) $-3^4$  (e) $5^0$  (f) $\left(\tfrac23\right)^3$  (g) $10^{-2}$  (h) $2^{-3}$  (i) $\sqrt{196}$  (j) $\sqrt{0.16}$  (k) $\sqrt{\tfrac{9}{25}}$  (l) $\dfrac{3^7}{3^4}$

**7.** (a) Write in scientific notation (*wetenschappelijke notatie*): $72\,300\,000$ and $0.000\,058$.
(b) Calculate and give the answer in scientific notation:
(i) $(2.5\times 10^6)\times(4\times 10^{-3})$  (ii) $\dfrac{9\times 10^5}{3\times 10^{-2}}$  (iii) $(6\times 10^4)^2$

**8.** Units and powers of 10.
(a) How many m² is 1 km²? Write it as a power of 10.
(b) How many litres is 1 m³? (1 litre = 1 dm³.)
(c) Write $5$ mm² in m², in scientific notation.

## School-exam level

**9.** A phone costs €400. Its price is raised by 20%, and later the new price is lowered by 20%.
(a) What is the final price?
(b) Would the answer change if the price were first lowered by 20% and then raised by 20%? Explain using growth factors.

**10.** A town has 50 000 inhabitants. The population grows by 3% per year.
(a) What is the growth factor per year?
(b) How many inhabitants are there after 5 years? Round to whole numbers.
(c) After how many whole years has the population first doubled? (Use your calculator to try values.)

**11.** You put €2000 in a savings account at 3% interest per year, and the interest is added to the account each year.
(a) How much is in the account after 5 years? Round to cents.
(b) How much would there be if you received 3% of the original €2000 each year, without interest on interest?

**12.** A bike costs €847 including 21% BTW (*btw*, VAT). What is the price excluding BTW, and how much BTW do you pay?

**13.** A walking map has scale $1 : 25\,000$.
(a) A path on the map is 6.4 cm long. How long is it in reality, in km?
(b) A lake on the map has area 3 cm². What is its real area in km²?

**14.** (a) The circumference of the Earth is about $4.0\times 10^7$ m and light travels at about $3.0\times 10^8$ m/s. How many times could light go round the Earth in one second?
(b) A human hair is about $8\times 10^{-5}$ m thick. How many hairs side by side make 1 cm?

**15.** You mix 200 ml of a drink that is 30% fruit juice with 300 ml of a drink that is 10% fruit juice. What percentage of the mixture is fruit juice? Why is the answer not 20%?

**16.** (a) Simplify $\sqrt{12} + \sqrt{27}$.
(b) Calculate $\sqrt{8}\cdot\sqrt{2}$ and $\dfrac{\sqrt{72}}{\sqrt{2}}$.
(c) Between which two whole numbers does $\sqrt{70}$ lie? Which is it closer to?
(d) Is $\sqrt{a^2} = a$ for every number $a$? Give an example or a reason.

**17.** Shop A offers 30% off. Shop B offers 20% off, and then a further 10% off the reduced price. Which shop is cheaper, and by what percentage of the original price?

## Challenge

**18.** (a) Prove that $\sqrt3$ is irrational. (Hint: copy the proof for $\sqrt2$, using "divisible by 3" instead of "even". You'll need: if $3 \mid a^2$ then $3 \mid a$. Why is that true?)
(b) Where exactly does the same proof break down if you try it for $\sqrt4$?

**19.** Your friend says: "$0.999\ldots$ is a tiny bit less than 1." Give two different arguments that $0.\overline{9} = 1$. Then give a third: if two numbers are different, there is a number between them. What number lies between $0.\overline{9}$ and $1$?

**20.** Prove: a fraction $\dfrac{a}{b}$ in lowest terms has a terminating decimal **if and only if** $b$ has no prime factors other than 2 and 5. (Prove both directions. For "only if", use that prime factorisations are unique.)

**21.** (a) Find the decimals of $\tfrac17, \tfrac27, \dots, \tfrac67$. What do you notice?
(b) Find the length of the repeating block (the *period*) of $\tfrac{1}{13}$ and of $\tfrac{1}{17}$. (Long division by hand, or a calculator with care.)
(c) Explain why the period of $\tfrac1n$ is at most $n - 1$.

**22.** No calculator. Which is larger?
(a) $2^{300}$ or $3^{200}$
(b) $31^{11}$ or $17^{14}$ (Hint: compare both with a power of 2.)

**23.** How many digits does $2^{100}$ have? Prove your answer without a calculator, using $2^{10} = 1024$. (You may use that $1.024^{10} < 1.3$; can you prove that too?)

**24.** A shop raises a price by $p\%$ and later lowers the new price by $p\%$. The final price is 96% of the original. Find $p$.

**25.** An *Egyptian fraction* is a sum of different unit fractions $\tfrac1n$.
(a) Show that $\dfrac{1}{n} = \dfrac{1}{n + 1} + \dfrac{1}{n(n+1)}$ for every whole number $n \ge 1$.
(b) Write $\tfrac27$ as a sum of two different unit fractions.
(c) Write $1$ as a sum of four different unit fractions.

**26.** The *mediant* of $\dfrac{a}{b}$ and $\dfrac{c}{d}$ (with $a, b, c, d$ positive) is $\dfrac{a + c}{b + d}$.
(a) Find the mediant of $\tfrac12$ and $\tfrac23$ and check that it lies between them.
(b) Prove: if $\dfrac{a}{b} < \dfrac{c}{d}$ then $\dfrac{a}{b} < \dfrac{a+c}{b+d} < \dfrac{c}{d}$.
(c) Deduce that between any two different fractions there are infinitely many fractions.
