# A2 Fractions, ratios, percentages and powers

[← Back to Stage A](../README.md)

**Stage:** A, groep 8 foundations
**School year:** groep 8 (2026–27); content from groep 8 rekenen, klas 1 and klas 2 (powers, roots, scientific notation)
**Length:** 6 weeks at about 4 hours a week (about 24 hours)
**Prerequisites:** [A1 Numbers and divisibility](../A1-numbers-divisibility/) (prime factorisation, gcd and lcm)
**Next:** [A3 First algebra](../A3-first-algebra/)

## Objectives

By the end of this module he can:

1. Calculate with fractions (*breuken*) in all four operations, and explain why each rule works, including "dividing by a fraction is multiplying by its reciprocal" (*omgekeerde*).
2. Convert between fractions and decimals (*decimale getallen*, *kommagetallen*), predict whether a decimal terminates or recurs (*repeterende breuk*), and turn a recurring decimal back into a fraction.
3. Work with ratios (*verhoudingen*) and the ratio table (*verhoudingstabel*), including map scales (*schaal*).
4. Calculate percentages (*procenten*) using the growth factor (*groeifactor*), including reverse percentages and repeated percentage change (*herhaalde procentuele verandering*), and explain why $+10\%$ then $-10\%$ is not zero change.
5. Use the rules for powers (*machten*) with whole-number exponents and explain why $a^0 = 1$; simplify square roots (*wortels*); prove that $\sqrt{2}$ is irrational.
6. Write and calculate with numbers in scientific notation (*wetenschappelijke notatie*).

## Curriculum mapping

- **Onderbouw kerndoelen:** 10 (*Getallen, grootheden en vergelijkingen*: rational numbers, ratios, percentages, powers and roots, scientific notation, measurement) and 14 (*Denk- en werkwijzen*: proof).
- **Upper-school exam programmes:** fluency with fractions, powers and roots is assumed throughout Wiskunde B domain A (*vaardigheden*) and wiskunde techniek A2 (*getallen en variabelen*). Repeated percentage change is the start of exponential growth (Stage B4, then C2).

## I can…

- [ ] add, subtract, multiply and divide fractions and mixed numbers without a calculator
- [ ] explain with a picture why $\tfrac23 \cdot \tfrac45 = \tfrac{8}{15}$ and why dividing by $\tfrac25$ is multiplying by $\tfrac52$
- [ ] say in advance whether a fraction gives a terminating decimal, and convert $0.\overline{36}$ into a fraction
- [ ] solve ratio problems with a ratio table, and use map scales for lengths and areas
- [ ] calculate a percentage increase or decrease with a growth factor, and undo one
- [ ] explain why a 10% rise followed by a 10% fall gives a 1% loss
- [ ] use the power rules and explain why $a^0 = 1$ and $10^{-3} = \tfrac{1}{1000}$
- [ ] simplify $\sqrt{50}$ and prove that $\sqrt{2}$ is irrational
- [ ] calculate with numbers in scientific notation

## Exit criteria

Move on to A3 when he can:

- do practice problems 1–8 accurately without a calculator (allowing at most two slips);
- solve at least 7 of the 9 school-exam problems;
- solve at least 4 challenge problems, including a written proof of problem 18 or 20;
- explain to a parent, without notes, why repeated percentage changes multiply.
