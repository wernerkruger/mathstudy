# A3 First algebra

[← Back to Stage A](../README.md)

**Stage:** A, groep 8 foundations
**School year:** groep 8 (2026–27); content from klas 1 (letters, formulas, graphs) and klas 2 (linear formulas, equations, brackets)
**Length:** 10 weeks at about 4 hours a week (about 40 hours)
**Prerequisites:** [A1 Numbers and divisibility](../A1-numbers-divisibility/) (negative numbers, order of operations) and [A2 Fractions, ratios, percentages and powers](../A2-fractions-ratios-powers/) (fractions, ratio tables, powers)
**Next:** [A4 Geometry and measurement](../A4-geometry-measurement/)

## Objectives

By the end of this module he can:

1. Use letters as variables (*variabelen*), write expressions (*uitdrukkingen*) and formulas (*formules*), and substitute values (*invullen*).
2. Simplify expressions (*herleiden*) by collecting like terms (*gelijksoortige termen*) and multiplying terms, and explain why $3a + 2b$ can't be simplified.
3. Expand brackets (*haakjes wegwerken*), including a minus sign before brackets and two pairs of brackets, and explain each step with the distributive law (*distributieve eigenschap*, *verdeelwet*).
4. Solve linear equations (*lineaire vergelijkingen*) with the balance method (*balansmethode*), including equations with brackets and fractions, and recognise equations with no solution or with every number as a solution.
5. Move between a formula, a table (*tabel*) and a graph (*grafiek*) of a linear relationship (*lineair verband*), using the starting value (*startgetal*) and the slope (*hellingsgetal*, *richtingscoëfficiënt*), and solve problems with two formulas by finding the point of intersection (*snijpunt*).
6. Find a direct formula for a pattern or arithmetic sequence (*rij*), and prove simple formulas such as $1 + 2 + \dots + n = \tfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$.
7. Translate word problems into equations, solve them and check the answer in the original story.
8. Solve simple linear inequalities (*ongelijkheden*) and explain why multiplying by a negative number reverses the inequality sign.

## Curriculum mapping

- **Onderbouw kerndoelen:** 10 (*Getallen, grootheden en vergelijkingen*: algebraic expressions, linear equations, inequalities) and 12 (*Patronen en verbanden*: sequences, formulas, tables, graphs, the function idea).
- **Upper-school exam programmes:** algebraic fluency is assessed in every exam question: Wiskunde B domain A (*vaardigheden*, especially algebraic skills) and B1 (*formules en functies*); wiskunde techniek A2 (*getallen en variabelen*) and A4 (*verbanden*).

## I can…

- [ ] substitute negative numbers and fractions into expressions correctly, such as $2a^2 - 3ab$ for $a = -2$, $b = 3$
- [ ] simplify expressions like $3p^2 + 2p - p^2 + p$ and $4x\cdot 3x$
- [ ] expand $-2(4 - 3a)$ and $(x + 3)(x + 5)$ and explain each step
- [ ] solve linear equations with brackets and fractions, and check the solution
- [ ] recognise when an equation has no solutions or infinitely many
- [ ] find the formula of a linear relationship from a table, a graph or two points
- [ ] find where two linear formulas give the same value, with a table, a graph and an equation
- [ ] rearrange a formula to make another letter the subject
- [ ] find a direct formula for a pattern and prove it
- [ ] turn a word problem into an equation, solve it and check it in the story
- [ ] solve an inequality and explain why the sign flips when multiplying by a negative number

## Exit criteria

Move on to A4 when he can:

- solve practice problems 1–8 fluently and accurately (at most two slips);
- solve at least 9 of the 11 school-exam problems, **writing the equation** before solving it;
- solve at least 5 of the challenge problems, including a written proof of problem 20, 25 or 26;
- solve a fresh linear equation with brackets and fractions in under 3 minutes, and check it.
