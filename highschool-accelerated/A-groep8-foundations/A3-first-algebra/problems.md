# A3 Problems: First algebra

[← Module overview](README.md) · [Notes](notes.md) · [Solutions](solutions.md)

No calculator unless stated. For every equation, **check** your solution. For word problems, say what your letter stands for, write the equation, solve it, and answer the question in a sentence.

---

## Practice

**1.** Simplify (*herleid*).
(a) $4a + 3b - 2a - 5b$
(b) $5x\cdot(-2x)$
(c) $3ab - 5ba + 2a$
(d) $2x^2 - 3x + 4x^2 + x - 7$
(e) $-3p\cdot 4q\cdot(-p)$
(f) $a\cdot a\cdot a + 2a^3$

**2.** Expand the brackets (*werk de haakjes weg*) and simplify.
(a) $4(3x - 2)$
(b) $-3(2 - 5a)$
(c) $7 - (4 - 3x)$
(d) $2(x + 5) - 3(x - 1)$
(e) $x(2x - 3) - x^2$
(f) $(x + 2)(x + 7)$
(g) $(2a - 1)(a + 4)$
(h) $(x - 3)^2$

**3.** Solve, and check your answer.
(a) $4x + 7 = 31$
(b) $7 - 2x = 3x - 8$
(c) $6x - 5 = 2x + 9$
(d) $4(x + 1) - 2(x - 3) = 20$
(e) $\dfrac{x}{4} + 2 = 5$
(f) $\dfrac{x + 3}{2} - \dfrac{x - 1}{3} = 3$

**4.** A plumber charges a €25 call-out fee plus €40 per hour.
(a) Write a formula for the cost $K$ (in euros) of a job of $t$ hours.
(b) Make a table for $t = 0, \tfrac12, 1, 2, 3$ and draw the graph.
(c) What does a job of $2\tfrac12$ hours cost?
(d) A bill is €165. How long did the job take?

**5.** The sequence $7, 11, 15, 19, \dots$ continues in the same way.
(a) Give a direct formula for term number $n$ (starting at $n = 1$).
(b) What is term 50?
(c) Is 403 a term of the sequence? Is 500?

**6.** Square tables are put in a row. One table seats 4 people, two tables in a row seat 6, three seat 8.
(a) How many people can sit at $n$ tables in a row? Explain your formula with a picture.
(b) How many tables are needed for 30 people?

**7.** Solve the inequalities and show the solution on a number line.
(a) $5x + 2 > 17$
(b) $4 - 3x \le 10$
(c) $2(x - 1) \ge 5x + 4$
(d) $\dfrac{x}{3} - 1 < 2$

**8.** Take $a = -3$ and $b = 2$. Calculate
(a) $a^2 - 2b$  (b) $-a^2 + ab$  (c) $(a + b)^2$  (d) $3a - 2(b - a)$  (e) $\dfrac{a - b}{a + b}$

## School-exam level

**9.** Phone plan A costs €12 a month plus €0.10 per minute of calling. Plan B costs €20 a month plus €0.05 per minute.
(a) Write a formula for the monthly cost of each plan, with $m$ the number of minutes.
(b) For how many minutes do the plans cost the same? Solve with an equation.
(c) Draw both graphs in one figure for $0 \le m \le 300$. Which plan is cheaper for someone who calls 250 minutes a month?

**10.** Anna is 4 years older than her brother. In 5 years their ages will add up to 40. How old are they now?

**11.** A rectangle has perimeter (*omtrek*) 46 cm. Its length is 5 cm more than its width. Find the length and the width.

**12.** The sum of four consecutive whole numbers is 154. Which numbers are they?

**13.** Two cyclists start at the same time from towns A and B, 60 km apart, and ride towards each other. The cyclist from A rides at 18 km/h, the one from B at 22 km/h. After how long do they meet, and how far from A?

**14.** Biologists noticed that crickets chirp faster when it is warm. A rule of thumb is $N = 7T - 30$, where $N$ is the number of chirps per minute and $T$ the temperature in °C.
(a) How many chirps per minute at 25 °C?
(b) Rewrite the formula as $T = \dots$.
(c) You count 110 chirps per minute. What is the temperature?
(d) Below what temperature does the formula say the crickets stop chirping? Round to one decimal.

**15.** Use Gauss's trick (notes §6) to calculate:
(a) $1 + 2 + 3 + \dots + 200$
(b) $2 + 4 + 6 + \dots + 200$
(c) $3 + 7 + 11 + 15 + \dots + 199$

**16.** A money box contains 25 coins, all 20-cent and 50-cent coins, worth €8.60 in total. How many of each are there?

**17.** A class trip costs €450 for the bus plus €12 entry per pupil. Each pupil pays €25. How many pupils must go for the money to cover the costs? Solve with an inequality.

**18.** A linear relationship has $y = 11$ when $x = 2$, and $y = 20$ when $x = 5$.
(a) Find the formula $y = ax + b$.
(b) Does the point $(10, 36)$ lie on the graph?
(c) For which $x$ is $y = 0$?

**19.** (a) Solve $3(x - 2) + 6 = 3x$. What do you notice?
(b) Solve $4x - 2(x + 1) = 2x + 5$. What do you notice?
(c) For which number $c$ is every $x$ a solution of $5x + c = 5(x + 2)$? What happens for other values of $c$?

## Challenge

**20.** (a) Calculate $1$, $1 + 3$, $1 + 3 + 5$, $1 + 3 + 5 + 7$. Guess a formula for $1 + 3 + 5 + \dots + (2n - 1)$.
(b) Prove your formula with Gauss's trick.
(c) Prove it again with a picture of dots arranged in a square.

**21.** Find all integers $n$ (positive, negative or zero) for which $\dfrac{2n + 7}{n + 1}$ is an integer. (Hint: write $2n + 7 = 2(n + 1) + \dots$)

**22.** (a) "Think of a number. Multiply it by 4. Add 12. Divide by 2. Subtract twice the number you started with." Prove that the answer is always 6.
(b) "Think of a number. Add 3. Multiply by the number you first thought of minus 3. Add 9." Prove that the result is always the square of your number.
(c) Invent a trick whose answer is always 5, and prove that it works.

**23.** (a) Explain with a picture of a square of side $a + b$ why $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + 2ab + b^2$.
(b) Use it to calculate $103^2$ and $98^2$ in your head.
(c) Use $(a + b)(a - b) = a^2 - b^2$ to calculate $97\cdot 103$ in your head.
(d) A student writes $(a + b)^2 = a^2 + b^2$. Find a picture or a pair of numbers that shows him what he forgot.

**24.** Pens cost €3 and notebooks €5. You want to spend exactly €41. Find all possibilities (whole numbers, possibly zero, of each).

**25.** (a) Show that the sum of the first $n$ terms of the arithmetic sequence $a, a + d, a + 2d, \dots$ is $\dfrac{n\,(2a + (n-1)d)}{2}$.
(b) Show that $2027$ can be written as a sum of (at least two) consecutive positive whole numbers.
(c) Prove that $64$ cannot be written as a sum of at least two consecutive positive whole numbers. (Hint: if the numbers are $m, m+1, \dots, m+k-1$, then by (a) $k(2m + k - 1) = 128$. Look at odd and even.)

**26.** Take any three-digit number with digits $a, b, c$ (so the number is $100a + 10b + c$), and reverse it. Prove that the difference between the number and its reverse is always divisible by 99. For which numbers is the difference 0?

**27.** You drive 60 km to your grandmother at 30 km/h and drive back at 60 km/h.
(a) What is your average speed for the whole trip? (It isn't 45 km/h!)
(b) Show that for any distance $d$ there and back, at speeds $u$ and $w$, the average speed is $\dfrac{2uw}{u + w}$.
(c) How fast would you have to drive back to make the average 60 km/h?

**28.** (a) Two numbers have sum 20 and difference 6. Find them without trial and error.
(b) Two numbers have sum $S$ and difference $D$. Find a formula for each number.

**29.** (a) Solve $3(x - 2) - 2(x + 1) > x - 10$. What happens?
(b) Give an inequality with no solutions at all, and explain why it has none.
