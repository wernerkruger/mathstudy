# A4 Geometry and measurement

[← Back to Stage A](../README.md)

**Stage:** A, groep 8 foundations
**School year:** groep 8 (2026–27); content from klas 1 (shapes, symmetry, area and volume, angles, solids) and klas 2 (Pythagoras)
**Length:** 10 weeks at about 4 hours a week (about 40 hours)
**Prerequisites:** [A1 Numbers and divisibility](../A1-numbers-divisibility/) (negative numbers for coordinates), [A2 Fractions, ratios, percentages and powers](../A2-fractions-ratios-powers/) (squares, square roots, π as a decimal), [A3 First algebra](../A3-first-algebra/) (formulas and equations)
**Next:** Stage B, [B3 Geometry: Pythagoras, similarity, trigonometry, proof](../../B-onderbouw-compact/)

## Objectives

By the end of this module he can:

1. Name and measure angles (*hoeken*: acute *scherp*, right *recht*, obtuse *stomp*, straight *gestrekt*), and use vertically opposite angles (*overstaande hoeken*), angles on a line (*nevenhoeken*), and corresponding and alternate angles at parallel lines (*F-hoeken*, *Z-hoeken*).
2. **Prove** that the angles of a triangle (*driehoek*) add up to 180°, and use this to find the angle sum of any polygon (*veelhoek*) and the angles of regular polygons (*regelmatige veelhoeken*).
3. Derive and use the formulas for the perimeter (*omtrek*) and area (*oppervlakte*) of rectangles, parallelograms, triangles and trapeziums, and the volume (*inhoud*) and surface area of prisms, cuboids (*balken*) and cylinders (*cilinders*).
4. Explain what π is, use the circumference and area of a circle (*cirkel*), and explain where the formula $A = \pi r^2$ comes from.
5. Recognise line symmetry (*lijnsymmetrie*), rotational symmetry (*draaisymmetrie*) and point symmetry (*puntsymmetrie*), and carry out reflections (*spiegelen*), rotations (*draaien*) and translations (*verschuiven*), including in coordinates (*coördinaten*).
6. Construct with compass (*passer*) and straightedge the perpendicular bisector (*middelloodlijn*), the angle bisector (*bissectrice*), a perpendicular and a 60° angle, and explain why each construction works.
7. State, **prove** and apply Pythagoras' theorem (*stelling van Pythagoras*), including distances in a coordinate system.

## Curriculum mapping

- **Onderbouw kerndoelen:** 13 (*Meetkunde*: 2D and 3D shapes, measurement, transformations; havo-vwo addition: reasoning with angles) and 14 (*Denk- en werkwijzen*: proof). Also 10 for measurement and units.
- **Upper-school exam programmes:** groundwork for Wiskunde B domain E (*meetkunde met coördinaten*: E1 *meetkundige vaardigheden*), for wiskunde techniek A6 (*meetkunde*) and B3 (*redeneren en bewijzen*, including plane-geometry proof), and for Wiskunde D geometry (Thales, circle theorems).

## I can…

- [ ] find unknown angles at intersecting and parallel lines, giving the reason for each step
- [ ] prove that the angles of a triangle add up to 180°
- [ ] find the angle sum of an $n$-gon and the angles of a regular $n$-gon, and explain why the exterior angles add up to 360°
- [ ] derive the area formulas for a parallelogram, triangle and trapezium from the rectangle
- [ ] explain what π is and why the area of a circle is $\pi r^2$
- [ ] calculate the volume and surface area of a cuboid, prism and cylinder, and convert between cm³, dm³, litres and m³
- [ ] find the lines of symmetry and the order of rotational symmetry of a shape
- [ ] reflect, rotate and translate points in a coordinate system and give the rule, such as $(x, y) \mapsto (-y, x)$
- [ ] construct a perpendicular bisector, an angle bisector and a 60° angle, and explain why they work
- [ ] prove Pythagoras' theorem and use it to find lengths and distances

## Exit criteria

Stage A is complete when he can:

- solve practice problems 1–9 accurately (at most two slips);
- solve at least 9 of the 11 school-exam problems, giving a reason for every angle;
- solve at least 5 challenge problems, including written proofs of problem 21 (Thales) and one other proof (22, 23 or 25);
- prove the triangle angle sum and Pythagoras' theorem aloud, with a sketch, without notes.
