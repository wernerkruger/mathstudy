# Stage A: groep 8 foundations

[← Back to the track overview](../README.md)

**When:** groep 8, school year 2026–27 (age 12)
**Length:** 32 weeks at about 4 hours a week (about 128 hours), October 2026 to early July 2027
**Next stage:** [B: klas 1, onderbouw compact](../B-onderbouw-compact/)

## What this stage is for

Stage A builds the number sense, first algebra and geometry that the whole track rests on. It covers roughly what Dutch gymnasium pupils meet in **klas 1 and the first half of klas 2**, but it treats it the way a strong mathematician would: every rule comes with a reason, and many come with a proof.

By the end of the stage he should:

- calculate fluently and accurately with integers, fractions, decimals, percentages and powers, without a calculator;
- understand *why* the rules of arithmetic work, including divisibility rules and the arithmetic of fractions;
- read, write and simplify algebraic expressions and solve linear equations confidently;
- know the basic facts of plane and solid geometry, and be able to prove some of them;
- have written his first real proofs: that there are infinitely many primes, that the angles of a triangle add up to 180°, that Pythagoras' theorem is true.

The modules are best done in this order: A2 uses the prime factorisations of A1, A3 uses the fractions of A2, and A4 uses the equations of A3. Within that, there is room for flexibility: A4's angle and symmetry sections need no algebra and can be mixed in earlier for variety.

The calendar leaves about 6 weeks of slack for holidays, the *doorstroomtoets* (the national groep 8 test, in February) and busy school weeks.

## How this relates to groep 8 school work

The groep 8 rekenen curriculum covers whole numbers, fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios, measurement and simple geometry, mostly as practical arithmetic. Stage A **includes all of that** and goes much further:

- At school he calculates; here he also explains *why* the calculation works.
- At school a ratio table (*verhoudingstabel*) is a calculation tool; here it is also the start of linear relationships and algebra.
- Negative numbers, algebra with letters, equations and proofs are not part of groep 8 at all.

So school work should feel easy. If it doesn't, slow down here and check basic fluency first. He should still take school work seriously: neat working and accuracy are habits that matter later in exams.

Ask the groep 8 teacher whether he may skip mastered rekenen work and use the time for this track. See the [parent guide](../PARENT_GUIDE.md#2-working-with-the-school).

## How this relates to the brugklas

In the brugklas (klas 1) at gymnasium he will probably use *Getal & Ruimte* or *Moderne Wiskunde*. A typical klas 1 year covers negative numbers, shapes and symmetry, graphs and formulas, calculating with letters, area and volume, angles, solids and statistics. Klas 2 adds linear formulas, equations, brackets, Pythagoras, quadratic formulas, similarity, powers and roots. (This order is typical but varies by school and method; check with the school.)

After Stage A he will have covered almost all of klas 1 and much of klas 2. Stage B then compresses the rest of the onderbouw into one year. That is why it is worth agreeing compacting with the school from the start of klas 1.

## Curriculum mapping

The onderbouw core targets (*kerndoelen*) that Stage A covers (new kerndoelen, law from August 2026; SLO explanatory document: <https://www.slo.nl/publish/pages/22237/definitieve-conceptkerndoelen-rekenen-wiskunde-toelichtingsdocument.pdf>):

| Kerndoel | Covered in |
|---|---|
| 10 Getallen, grootheden en vergelijkingen (numbers, quantities, equations) | A1, A2, A3 |
| 12 Patronen en verbanden (patterns and relationships) | A3 |
| 13 Meetkunde (geometry) | A4 |
| 14 Denk- en werkwijzen (mathematical thinking, including proof) | A1, A4 and all challenge sections |
| 15 Wiskundetaal en gereedschap (mathematical language and tools) | all modules |

Stage A does not yet cover kerndoel 11 (data and probability); that comes in B5.

In the upper-school exam programmes, Stage A is groundwork for Wiskunde B domain A (*vaardigheden*, skills: algebraic fluency, reasoning) and for wiskunde techniek A2 (*getallen en variabelen*) and B3 (*redeneren en bewijzen*, reasoning and proof).

## How to work through a module

1. Read the module's `README.md` for the plan.
2. Read `notes.md` a section at a time. Try each worked example before reading its solution.
3. Do the problems in `problems.md` in order: Practice, then School-exam level, then Challenge.
4. Check with `solutions.md` only after a real attempt. For challenge problems, wait at least a day before looking.
5. Tick the "I can…" checklist in the module README and update [PROGRESS.md](../PROGRESS.md).
