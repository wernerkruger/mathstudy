# Stage E: Wiskunde D and exam preparation (klas 4, 2030–31)

[← Track overview](../README.md) · [Glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) · [Progress](../PROGRESS.md)

**When:** klas 4 at gymnasium, school year 2030–31, age about 16.
**Length:** 38 weeks at about 4 hours a week, on top of school.
**Previous stage:** [D Bovenbouw advanced](../D-bovenbouw-advanced/) (klas 3). **Next:** [F Beyond school](../F-beyond-school/) (klas 5–6).

At the start of klas 4 he has covered the core of the upper-school programme (*bovenbouw*): functions, calculus, trigonometry, vectors, probability. Stage E adds the topics of **current Wiskunde D** and the **additions in wiskunde techniek**, and ends with systematic practice on past central exams. By the end of this year he has covered every topic in current Wiskunde B, wiskunde techniek and current Wiskunde D, and is ready for an early central exam.

## Planning a possible early central exam in klas 5 (May 2032)

From the verified research for this track:

- Dutch law (WVO 2020, art. 2.56, lid 4) lets a school allow a pupil to sit the central exam in a subject **one or two years early**, in VWO klas 4 or klas 5.
- The **school and the subject teacher must agree**. The **school exam (*schoolexamen*, SE) for that subject must be completed first**. Results of an early exam **lapse if the pupil is not promoted** to the next year.
- He started klas 4 in September 2030, one year after the new programmes start (klas 4 in September 2029). The first VWO central exam under the new programme is **probably in May 2032** (an inference: the 2029 klas-4 cohort finishes VWO 6 in 2032). So **klas 5, May 2032**, is the realistic early date: he would sit the same new exam as the first regular cohort. An exam in klas 4 (May 2031) would still be the old Wiskunde B programme, which he does not follow.

**What to do in klas 4 (this year):**

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| September–October 2030 | Parent and pupil meet the mathematics teacher and the *decaan* (careers/exam coordinator). Ask: does the school offer wiskunde techniek and the Wiskunde D successor? Would it allow an early CE in klas 5? What does the *PTA* (*programma van toetsing en afsluiting*, the school-exam schedule) require, and can the SE tests be taken earlier or compressed? |
| January 2031 | Show the school evidence: completed modules, E1–E3 tests, and (by then) olympiad results. Ask for a written agreement on the early-exam route. |
| April–June 2031 | E4 exam practice. Scores of 8+ on timed past papers are the strongest argument for the school. |
| June 2031 | Decide together with the school: early CE in May 2032 (klas 5) yes or no. If yes, agree an SE plan for klas 5 that finishes before the CE. |

**Points to weigh** (see also the [parent guide](../PARENT_GUIDE.md)):

- An early CE frees up time in klas 6 for university courses ([stage F](../F-beyond-school/)) and for the *profielwerkstuk*.
- The first exam of a new programme carries some risk: fewer sample papers, possible adjustments. He will have covered everything in all programmes, which limits that risk.
- Wiskunde D (and its successor) has **no central exam**: it is completed through the school exam only, so "early" there means agreeing an earlier SE schedule with the school.
- There is no grade advantage in being early; the gain is time.

## Weekly rhythm

About 4 hours a week, for example: two sessions of 1.5 hours (reading notes, worked examples, practice problems) and one session of 1 hour (challenge problem or programming). The olympiad track continues alongside at about 1 hour a week ([olympiad](../olympiad/)); in January (first round) and March (second round) shift time towards it.

## Checkpoints

- After E1: timed module test, 75% or more.
- After E2: timed module test, 75% or more.
- After E3: timed module test, 75% or more; two proofs reviewed by a teacher.
- After E4: grade 8+ on three of the last four timed papers.
