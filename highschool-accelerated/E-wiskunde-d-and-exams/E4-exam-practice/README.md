# E4 Exam practice

[← Stage E overview](../README.md) · [Exam technique](exam-technique.md) · [Mock paper](mock-paper.md) · [Mock paper solutions](mock-paper-solutions.md)

**Stage:** E, klas 4 (school year 2030–31)
**Length:** 12 weeks at about 4 hours a week
**Goal:** be able to score a high grade (8 or more) on a VWO central exam (*centraal examen*, CE) in mathematics under real conditions, a year before a possible early exam in klas 5 (May 2032).

This module has no new mathematics. It turns knowledge into exam performance: speed, accuracy, writing down the right things, and using the graphing calculator well.

## Prerequisites

All of stages C and D and modules E1–E3:

- [C Bovenbouw core](../../C-bovenbouw-core/): functions, exponentials and logarithms, differential calculus, trigonometric functions
- [D Bovenbouw advanced](../../D-bovenbouw-advanced/): differential calculus II, [D2 Integral calculus](../../D-bovenbouw-advanced/D2-integral-calculus/), analytic geometry and vectors, probability and statistics
- [E1](../E1-differential-equations/), [E2](../E2-complex-numbers/), [E3](../E3-geometry-proof-conics/)

## Which exams to use

**Source.** Past VWO Wiskunde B central exams with their marking schemes (*correctievoorschrift*) and any later corrections (*aanvulling op het correctievoorschrift*) are collected on alleexamens.nl, back to 1999:
<https://www.alleexamens.nl/examens/VWO/Wiskunde-B/>
The official source is examenblad.nl (it blocks automated access, so check it in a browser).

**Which years.** Use exams from **2018 onwards** first: they belong to the Wiskunde B programme that started in 2015 and are closest to wiskunde techniek. There are normally two papers a year, *tijdvak 1* (May) and *tijdvak 2* (June, the resit). There was no central exam in 2020. Older exams (before 2018) are still good practice for calculus and trigonometry, but skip questions on topics outside the current programmes.

**Wiskunde techniek.** By 2030 the first wiskunde techniek exam will not have been sat yet (probably May 2032 for the cohort that started klas 4 in 2029). Check whether the CvTE has published *proeve van bekwaamheid* or sample exams (*voorbeeldexamens*) for wiskunde techniek on examenblad.nl or on the SLO site <https://www.actualisatie-examenprogrammas.nl/wiskunde-havo-vwo>. If so, do them in weeks 10–11 instead of old Wiskunde B papers.

## How to sit a past paper

1. **Conditions.** 3 hours (180 minutes), a quiet room, phone in another room, only the graphing calculator, ruler, compass, protractor and the paper's formula sheet. Print the paper; write on A4 paper as in the real exam.
2. **Calculator.** In the real exam the graphing calculator (*grafische rekenmachine*, GR) must be a model on the CvTE's list of allowed models and must be in exam mode (*examenstand*), which disables stored programs and files. The list and the exact rules are in the yearly *Regeling toegestane hulpmiddelen* on examenblad.nl. Check the rules **every year**: the new programmes put more weight on *digitaal gereedschap*, and the rules for 2032 may differ (for example, a computer environment instead of a handheld GR). Practise with the exact model and mode you will use.
3. **First pass (about 10 minutes).** Read the whole paper. Mark each question as *quick*, *standard* or *hard*.
4. **Time budget.** Roughly 2.4 minutes per point (180 minutes for about 75 points). A 4-point question gets about 10 minutes. If you are stuck after twice the budget, write down what you have, mark it, and move on.
5. **Writing.** Follow [exam technique](exam-technique.md): working, calculator input, exact vs approximate, rounding at the end.
6. **Stop at 180 minutes.** Then, separately, you may finish unfinished questions in a different colour for learning, but score only the timed work.

## Scoring with the correctievoorschrift

- Mark strictly, as a second marker (*tweede corrector*) would. Each bullet in the scheme earns its points only if that step is visibly on paper.
- Apply the general rules at the top of the *correctievoorschrift* (deductions for missing working, wrong rounding, calculator use without explanation).
- Check the *aanvulling* (correction notice) for that year, if there is one.
- Compute the grade with the formula $9\cdot\frac{S}{L} + N$, with $S$ your score, $L$ the maximum score and $N$ the N-term the CvTE published for that paper (in the *normering* on examenblad.nl; if you cannot find it, use $N = 1$). The parent should ideally mark the first two papers alongside him and compare.

## Error log

Keep one table (spreadsheet or notebook) for every point lost:

| Date | Paper (year, tijdvak) | Question | Points lost | Topic | Type of error | What I will do differently |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2031-03-02 | 2024-I | 7 | 2 of 5 | trig equations | missed the second solution in $[0, 2\pi]$ | always draw the unit circle and list all solutions |

**Types of error:**

- **K** knowledge: did not know the method or formula
- **R** reading: misread the question or answered a different question
- **C** calculation: algebra or arithmetic slip
- **W** writing: correct answer but working or reasoning missing, so points lost
- **G** graphing calculator: wrong input, window, mode (degrees vs radians), or input not written down
- **T** time: did not reach the question

Every two weeks, count by type and topic. If one type dominates, fix that first: W and G errors are the cheapest points to win back.

## 12-week schedule

About 4 hours a week. "Paper" means a full timed past paper (3 hours) plus marking (about 1 hour).

| Week | Activity |
|---|---|
| 1 | Read [exam technique](exam-technique.md). Calculator skills session: solving equations (intersect, zero), max/min, numerical derivative and integral, tables, normalcdf/binomcdf, exam mode. Do the 2018 tijdvak 1 paper untimed with the scheme open; start the error log. |
| 2 | Paper: 2019 tijdvak 1 (timed). Mark. |
| 3 | Paper: 2021 tijdvak 1. Mark. Review the error log; redo every question with lost points. |
| 4 | Topic week: the weakest topic from the error log. Collect all questions on that topic from 2018–2025 papers and do them in a row. |
| 5 | Paper: 2022 tijdvak 1. Mark. |
| 6 | Paper: 2023 tijdvak 1. Mark. |
| 7 | Topic week: probability and differential equations (wiskunde techniek additions): E1 problems 11, 12, 17; probability questions from D4 and from VWO Wiskunde A exams. |
| 8 | Paper: 2024 tijdvak 1. Mark. Error log review. |
| 9 | The [mock paper](mock-paper.md) in this folder (timed). Mark with [the solutions](mock-paper-solutions.md). |
| 10 | Paper: most recent tijdvak 1 available, or a wiskunde techniek sample exam if published. |
| 11 | Paper: a tijdvak 2 paper (resit papers are often slightly harder). |
| 12 | Final review: redo the ten questions from the error log with the most points lost. Write a one-page personal checklist from the log. |

If he scores 8+ on three papers in a row, the remaining paper weeks can be used for [stage F](../../F-beyond-school/) material instead.

## "I can…" checklist

- [ ] I can finish a 3-hour paper in time, with at most 10 minutes of unfinished work.
- [ ] I know the meaning of each command word (*bereken*, *bereken exact*, *toon aan*, *bewijs*, *benader*, *beredeneer*) and what working it requires.
- [ ] I write down calculator input for every *bereken* question.
- [ ] I round only at the end, to the precision asked.
- [ ] I can do every standard calculator operation in exam mode without hesitation.
- [ ] I keep and use an error log; my W and G errors have gone down.
- [ ] I have scored 8 or more on at least three timed papers.

## Exit criteria

- Grade 8.0 or higher (with the official N-term) on three of the last four timed papers, including the mock paper.
- Error log shows no repeated W (writing) or G (calculator) errors in the last three papers.
- He and the parent have discussed with the school whether to sit the CE early in klas 5 (May 2032); see [stage F](../../F-beyond-school/).
