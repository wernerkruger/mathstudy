# E3 Geometry, proof and conic sections

[← Stage E overview](../README.md) · [Notes](notes.md) · [Problems](problems.md) · [Solutions](solutions.md)

**Stage:** E, klas 4 (school year 2030–31)
**Length:** 10 weeks at about 4 hours a week
**Exam programmes:** current Wiskunde D domain D (*meetkunde*); wiskunde techniek A6 (*meetkunde*) and B3 (*redeneren en bewijzen*: synthetic and analytic proof in plane geometry, new compared with Wiskunde B); Wiskunde B domain E (*meetkunde met coördinaten*) for lines, circles and the inner product.

## Prerequisites

- Analytic geometry in the plane, vectors and the inner product (*inproduct*): [D3 Analytic geometry and vectors](../../D-bovenbouw-advanced/D3-analytic-geometry-vectors/)
- Congruence and similarity proofs, angles with parallel lines: [B3 Geometry and trigonometry](../../B-onderbouw-compact/B3-geometry-trigonometry/)
- Implicit differentiation (*impliciet differentiëren*) for tangent lines to conics: [D1 Differential calculus II](../../D-bovenbouw-advanced/D1-differential-calculus-II/); integrals from [D2](../../D-bovenbouw-advanced/D2-integral-calculus/) are not needed here
- The [olympiad geometry module O4](../../olympiad/O4-olympiad-geometry/) is a natural companion

## Objectives

By the end of this module he can:

1. Write a synthetic proof (*bewijs*) using given facts (*gegeven*), what is to be proved (*te bewijzen*) and justified steps, with angle chasing (*hoeken berekenen*), congruence (*congruentie*) and similarity (*gelijkvormigheid*).
2. State, prove and use Thales' theorem (*stelling van Thales*) and its converse.
3. State, prove (all three cases) and use the inscribed angle theorem (*stelling van de omtrekshoek*), including the central angle (*middelpuntshoek*) and the angle between tangent and chord (*hoek tussen koorde en raaklijn*).
4. Prove and use the properties of a cyclic quadrilateral (*koordenvierhoek*) and its converse.
5. Prove geometric statements analytically with coordinates or vectors, and choose a convenient coordinate system.
6. Determine loci (*meetkundige plaatsen*): perpendicular bisector (*middelloodlijn*), angle bisector (*bissectrice*), circle of Apollonius, and the conics.
7. Define the parabola (*parabool*), ellipse (*ellips*) and hyperbola (*hyperbool*) with focus (*brandpunt*) and directrix (*richtlijn*) or directrix circle (*richtcirkel*), derive their standard equations, and prove their reflection properties (*raaklijneigenschappen*).
8. Calculate in space with vectors: inner product, cross product (*uitproduct*, *vectorproduct*), normal vector (*normaalvector*), equation of a plane (*vergelijking van een vlak*), angles and distances.

## "I can…" checklist

- [ ] I can write a proof with *gegeven*, *te bewijzen*, *bewijs*, and justify every step with a named theorem.
- [ ] I can prove Thales' theorem and its converse.
- [ ] I can prove the inscribed angle theorem, including the case where the centre lies outside the angle.
- [ ] I can prove that opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral add up to $180°$, and use the converse to show four points are concyclic.
- [ ] I can use the tangent–chord angle theorem.
- [ ] I can set up coordinates cleverly and prove a theorem analytically.
- [ ] I can find the equation of a locus and recognise the curve.
- [ ] I can derive the equations of parabola, ellipse and hyperbola from their focus definitions.
- [ ] I can prove the reflection property of the parabola and the ellipse.
- [ ] I can compute a cross product and use it for normal vectors, areas and volumes.
- [ ] I can find the equation of a plane through three points, the angle between planes and the distance from a point to a plane.

## Exit criteria

Move on to E4 when:

- He scores at least 75% on the timed test (problems 12, 16, 19, 22).
- He can produce, on a blank sheet, complete proofs of Thales' theorem, the inscribed angle theorem and the parabola's reflection property.
- A teacher or parent has read two of his written proofs and found every step justified.
