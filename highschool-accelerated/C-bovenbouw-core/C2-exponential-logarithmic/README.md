# C2 Exponential and logarithmic functions

[← Stage C overview](../README.md) · Previous: [C1 Functions, graphs and equations](../C1-functions-graphs/) · Next: [C3 Differential calculus I](../C3-differential-calculus-I/)

**Stage:** C, upper-school core · **School year:** klas 2 (2028–29) · **Length:** 7 weeks at about 4 hours a week

## Prerequisites

- [C1 Functions, graphs and equations](../C1-functions-graphs/): transformations, inverse functions, solving inequalities with a graph.
- [B4 Powers, roots and exponential growth](../../B-onderbouw-compact/B4-powers-exponential-growth/): power rules, growth factor (*groeifactor*), percentages, exponential growth tables.

## Objectives

By the end of this module he can:

1. Work with exponential functions (*exponentiële functies*) $f(x)=b\cdot g^x$: growth factor, initial value, horizontal asymptote, transformations.
2. Define the logarithm (*logaritme*) ${}^g\!\log x$ as the inverse of $g^x$ and compute simple logarithms exactly.
3. State **and prove** the rules of logarithms (*rekenregels voor logaritmen*): product, quotient, power, and change of base (*overgaan op een ander grondtal*).
4. Explain where the number $e$ comes from and work with the natural logarithm (*natuurlijke logaritme*) $\ln x$.
5. Solve exponential and logarithmic equations and inequalities (*exponentiële en logaritmische vergelijkingen en ongelijkheden*) exactly, including substitution ($u = 2^x$) and checking the domain.
6. Convert growth factors between time units, and compute half-life (*halveringstijd*) and doubling time (*verdubbelingstijd*).
7. Read and use logarithmic scales (*logaritmische schaal*): decibels, pH, earthquake magnitudes, semi-log graphs (*enkellogaritmisch papier*).

## Exam mapping

- **Wiskunde B (CvTE syllabus 2028):** B2 standaardfuncties (exponential and logarithmic functions, including $e^x$ and $\ln x$), B4 inverse functies (log as inverse of exponential), B5 vergelijkingen en ongelijkheden (exact solving with logarithms), with skills from domain A. Derivatives of $e^x$ and $\ln x$ come later, in stage D (Wiskunde B subdomain C2).
- **Wiskunde techniek (concept 2026):** A4 verbanden (exponential and logarithmic functions), A5 veranderingen (growth processes), B2 onderzoeken met modellen.

## I can…

- [ ] compute logarithms like ${}^4\!\log 8$ and ${}^{1/3}\!\log 27$ exactly
- [ ] prove ${}^g\!\log(ab)={}^g\!\log a+{}^g\!\log b$ and the power and change-of-base rules
- [ ] convert a growth factor per year into one per month or per decade
- [ ] find the half-life or doubling time exactly and approximately
- [ ] explain where $e$ comes from and rewrite $b\cdot g^t$ as $b\cdot e^{kt}$
- [ ] solve equations such as $4^x-6\cdot2^x+8=0$ and ${}^3\!\log x+{}^3\!\log(x-8)=2$, checking the domain
- [ ] solve inequalities such as $0.8^x<0.3$ and say why the inequality sign may flip
- [ ] find the inverse of a transformed exponential function
- [ ] read a semi-log graph and turn a straight line on it into an exponential formula
- [ ] calculate with decibels and pH

## Exit criteria

- At least 80% of the Practice and CE-level problems solved correctly without the solutions.
- At least two Challenge problems solved, including problem 20 (irrationality of ${}^2\!\log 3$).
- He can prove the product rule and the change-of-base rule for logarithms on a blank sheet.
- He can do two past CE questions on exponential or logarithmic functions within exam time.
