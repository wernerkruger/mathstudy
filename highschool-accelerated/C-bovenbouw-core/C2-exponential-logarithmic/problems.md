# C2 Problems: exponential and logarithmic functions

[← Module README](README.md) · [Notes](notes.md) · [Solutions](solutions.md)

"Bereken exact" means no decimal approximations. Where an approximation is asked, you may use a calculator, but write down the exact expression you are evaluating.

---

## Practice

**1.** Compute exactly.
a. ${}^2\!\log64$  b. ${}^5\!\log\frac{1}{125}$  c. ${}^8\!\log4$  d. ${}^{1/3}\!\log27$  e. $\ln\left(e^2\sqrt e\right)$  f. $\log0.0001$

**2.** Simplify.
a. ${}^2\!\log12+{}^2\!\log6-2\cdot{}^2\!\log3$
b. $3\ln x-\ln(x^2)+\ln\left(\dfrac4x\right)$ for $x>0$

**3.** Solve exactly.
a. $3^{x+1}=81$  b. $9^x=27^{x-2}$  c. $7^x=20$ (also give a two-decimal approximation)  d. $2^x\cdot5^x=0.01$

**4.** Solve exactly.
a. ${}^3\!\log(2x-1)=2$
b. ${}^2\!\log x+{}^2\!\log(x+2)=3$

**5.** Solve exactly: $9^x-4\cdot3^x+3=0$.

**6.** Caesium-137 has a half-life of 30 years.
a. Find the growth factor per year (four decimals).
b. How long does it take until 5% of a quantity is left? Round to whole years.

**7.** A savings account pays 6% interest per year.
a. Compute the doubling time exactly and rounded to two decimals.
b. Compare with the "rule of 70" from the notes.

**8.** Given $f(x)=3\cdot2^{x+1}-12$.
a. Give the horizontal asymptote and the range.
b. Bereken exact the zero of $f$.
c. Find $f^{-1}(x)$.

**9.** Solve exactly.
a. $e^{3x}=7$
b. $\ln x+\ln(x-2)=\ln8$

**10.** Lemon juice has pH 2.5; rain water has pH 6.0. How many times as large is the hydrogen-ion concentration in lemon juice? Give an exact expression and an approximation.

**11.** Solve.
a. $0.6^x>0.2$ (give the boundary exactly and to two decimals)
b. ${}^{1/3}\!\log(x+2)<-1$ (exactly)

## CE-level

**12.** Given $f(x)=\ln(9-x^2)$.
a. Give the domain of $f$.
b. Solve exactly: $f(x)=\ln5$.
c. Solve exactly: $f(x)\ge\ln8$.
d. Toon aan that the maximum value of $f$ is $2\ln3$.

**13.** Given $f(x)=\ln x$ and $g(x)=\ln(4-x)$.
a. Toon aan that the graph of $g$ is the reflection of the graph of $f$ in the line $x=2$.
b. Bereken exact the coordinates of the intersection point of the graphs.
c. Solve exactly: $f(x)+g(x)=\ln3$.

**14.** After an injection, the concentration of a medicine in the blood is $C(t)=12\cdot0.85^t$ mg/L, $t$ in hours.
a. Calculate the half-life in hours, rounded to two decimals.
b. Write $C$ in the form $C(t)=12\cdot e^{-kt}$. Give $k$ exactly and to four decimals.
c. The medicine works as long as $C>1$. Calculate how long it works, in hours and minutes.

**15.**
a. Prove that ${}^a\!\log b\cdot{}^b\!\log c={}^a\!\log c$ for positive $a,b,c$ with $a,b\neq1$.
b. Compute exactly: ${}^2\!\log3\cdot{}^3\!\log4\cdot{}^4\!\log5\cdots{}^{15}\!\log16$.

**16.** Given $f(x)=e^{2x}-4e^x$.
a. Bereken exact the zero of $f$.
b. Solve exactly: $f(x)=5$.
c. Solve exactly: $f(x)<0$.

**17.** The sound level of one machine is 80 dB. Sound intensities of machines add up. Use $L=10\cdot\log\dfrac I{I_0}$.
a. Calculate the sound level of 2 identical machines, to one decimal.
b. How many identical machines give 100 dB?

**18.** The energy $E$ released by an earthquake is related to the magnitude $M$ by $M=\frac23\log\dfrac E{E_0}$.
a. Toon aan that when $M$ increases by 1, the energy is multiplied by $10^{1.5}$.
b. How many times as much energy does a magnitude 7.2 earthquake release as one of magnitude 5.8?

**19.** In a lab experiment, $\log N$ is plotted against $t$ (in hours). The points lie on the line through $(0,\,1.5)$ and $(5,\,4)$.
a. Find the formula $N=b\cdot g^t$. Give $b$ and $g$ exactly and to two decimals.
b. Calculate the doubling time in minutes.

## Challenge

**20.** Prove that ${}^2\!\log3$ is irrational.

**21.** Which is larger, $31^{11}$ or $17^{14}$? Decide without a calculator.

**22.** Solve: $x^{\log x}=100x$ (base-10 logarithm).

**23.** Consider the equation $2^x=x^2$.
a. Find two positive integer solutions.
b. Show that there is also a negative solution, and find it to two decimals with a calculator.
c. (Harder) Explain why there are no further solutions. You may use a graph, but give reasons why the graph can be trusted.

**24.** Solve exactly: ${}^x\!\log2+{}^2\!\log x=\tfrac52$.

**25.** Carbon-14 has a half-life of 5730 years. A piece of wood contains 30% of the carbon-14 that living wood contains. Estimate its age, rounded to tens of years.
