# C1 Functions, graphs and equations

[← Stage C overview](../README.md) · Next: [C2 Exponential and logarithmic functions](../C2-exponential-logarithmic/)

**Stage:** C, upper-school core · **School year:** klas 2 (2028–29) · **Length:** 9 weeks at about 4 hours a week

## Prerequisites

- [B1 Linear relations and equations](../../B-onderbouw-compact/B1-linear-relations/): slope, lines, linear equations and inequalities.
- [B2 Algebra and quadratics](../../B-onderbouw-compact/B2-algebra-quadratics/): expanding, factorising, the quadratic formula (*abc-formule*), parabolas.
- [B4 Powers, roots and exponential growth](../../B-onderbouw-compact/B4-powers-exponential-growth/): rules for powers, roots, fractional exponents.

## Objectives

By the end of this module he can:

1. Use the formal function concept (*functie*): input, output, function notation $f(x)$, domain (*domein*) and range (*bereik*), and find both for a given formula.
2. Recognise and sketch the standard functions (*standaardfuncties*): linear, quadratic, power functions $x^n$, root function $\sqrt{x}$, reciprocal $\tfrac{1}{x}$, absolute value $\lvert x\rvert$.
3. Describe and apply transformations (*transformaties*): translation (*translatie*, *verschuiving*), vertical and horizontal stretch (*vermenigvuldiging ten opzichte van de x-as / y-as*), reflection (*spiegeling*); find the formula of the image graph and vice versa.
4. Find the inverse function (*inverse functie*) of a one-to-one function, including restricting the domain, and relate its graph to the original by reflection in $y=x$.
5. Solve equations (*vergelijkingen*) and inequalities (*ongelijkheden*) algebraically (exactly) and graphically, including root equations (with checking for false solutions) and rational equations.
6. Find vertical, horizontal and oblique asymptotes (*verticale, horizontale, scheve asymptoot*) and describe limit behaviour (*limietgedrag*) of rational and root functions; find a hole (*perforatie*).
7. Work with absolute value (*absolute waarde*) in equations, inequalities and graphs.
8. Recognise even and odd functions (symmetry in the $y$-axis and in the origin).

## Exam mapping

- **Wiskunde B (CvTE syllabus 2028):** domain B Functies, grafieken en vergelijkingen: B1 formules en functies, B2 standaardfuncties, B3 functies en grafieken (transformations), B4 inverse functies, B5 vergelijkingen en ongelijkheden, B6 asymptoten en limietgedrag. Skills from domain A (vaardigheden), especially exact algebra.
- **Wiskunde techniek (concept 2026):** A4 verbanden (functions, standard functions, transformations, inverse functions, equations) and A2 getallen en variabelen (algebraic manipulation); B3 redeneren en bewijzen.

## I can…

- [ ] find the domain and range of a function such as $f(x)=1+\sqrt{6-2x}$ and justify the range
- [ ] sketch all standard functions from memory and state their domain, range and symmetry
- [ ] give the formula of a graph after a sequence of transformations, in the correct order
- [ ] describe the transformations that turn $y=\sqrt{x}$ into $y=-2\sqrt{x+3}+5$
- [ ] find the inverse of a one-to-one function and state its domain
- [ ] explain why the graph of $f^{-1}$ is the reflection of the graph of $f$ in the line $y=x$
- [ ] solve root equations and check for false solutions
- [ ] solve rational inequalities with a sign diagram
- [ ] find vertical, horizontal and oblique asymptotes and a hole (*perforatie*)
- [ ] solve equations and inequalities with absolute values by splitting into cases
- [ ] write a CE-style "toon aan" answer that a marker would give full points

## Exit criteria

- At least 80% of the Practice and CE-level problems solved correctly without looking at the solutions.
- At least three Challenge problems solved.
- He can do two past CE questions from domain B (without derivatives) in the time the exam allows (about 3 minutes per point).
- He can explain to an adult, without notes, why a root equation can give a false solution and why $f^{-1}$ is a reflection in $y=x$.
