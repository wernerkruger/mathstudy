# C1 Problems: functions, graphs and equations

[← Module README](README.md) · [Notes](notes.md) · [Solutions](solutions.md)

Work exactly unless a problem says you may use a graphing calculator (GR). "Bereken exact" means: no decimal approximations.

---

## Practice

**1.** Give the domain and the range of
a. $f(x) = 3 - \sqrt{8-4x}$
b. $g(x) = \dfrac{1}{x^2-9}$

**2.** Given $g(x)=\dfrac{3x-2}{x+1}$.
a. Give the domain of $g$.
b. Give the equations of the asymptotes.
c. What is the range of $g$?

**3.** Describe a sequence of transformations that turns the graph of $y=\dfrac1x$ into the graph of $y = 2 - \dfrac{3}{x+1}$.

**4.** The graph of $y=\sqrt{x}$ is first stretched from the $y$-axis by factor $\tfrac12$ and then translated by $(3,-1)$.
a. Give the formula of the image.
b. Give the formula if the two transformations are done in the opposite order. Is the result the same graph?

**5.** Find the inverse function of $g(x) = \dfrac{3x-2}{x+1}$ and give its domain.

**6.** Solve exactly.
a. $\lvert 3x+2\rvert = 8$
b. $\lvert 3x+2\rvert \ge 8$

**7.** Solve exactly: $x^3 - x^2 - 6x > 0$.

**8.** Solve exactly, and check for false solutions.
a. $\sqrt{2x+9} = x+3$
b. $\sqrt{x+5} = x-1$

**9.** Given $f(x) = \dfrac{2x^2+1}{x^2+1}$.
a. Show that $f(x) = 2 - \dfrac{1}{x^2+1}$.
b. Give the horizontal asymptote and the range of $f$.

**10.** Decide whether each function is even, odd or neither. Prove your answer.
a. $f(x)=x^3-x$  b. $g(x)=x^4+x^2$  c. $h(x)=x^2+x$  d. $k(x)=\dfrac{x}{x^2+1}$

**11.** Given $f(x)=x^2+6x+5$ with domain $x\le -3$. Find $f^{-1}$ and its domain.

## CE-level

**12.** Given $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-9}{x^2+x-6}$.
a. Toon aan that the graph of $f$ has exactly one vertical asymptote.
b. The graph of $f$ has a hole (*perforatie*). Bereken exact its coordinates.
c. Give the horizontal asymptote.

**13.** Given $f(x)=\sqrt{x+4}$ and $g(x)=\tfrac12x+2$.
a. Bereken exact the $x$-coordinates of the intersection points of the graphs of $f$ and $g$.
b. Solve exactly: $f(x) > g(x)$.

**14.** Given $f(x)=\dfrac1x$ and the lines $\ell_p: y = -x + p$.
Investigate, depending on $p$, how many intersection points the graph of $f$ and $\ell_p$ have. Give the values of $p$ for which $\ell_p$ touches the graph exactly.

**15.** Given $f(x) = 1 + \sqrt{x-1}$.
a. Give the domain and range of $f$.
b. Toon aan that $f^{-1}(x) = (x-1)^2 + 1$, and give the domain of $f^{-1}$.
c. Bereken exact the coordinates of the intersection points of the graphs of $f$ and $f^{-1}$.

**16.** Given $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-3x+5}{x-2}$.
a. Toon aan that $f(x)= x - 1 + \dfrac{3}{x-2}$.
b. Give the equations of all asymptotes of the graph of $f$.

**17.** Solve exactly: $\dfrac{2x-1}{x+3} \le 1$.

**18.** Solve exactly: $\lvert x^2-2x\rvert = x$.

**19.** Given $f(x)=x^2+4x$. The graph of $g$ arises from the graph of $f$ by a stretch from the $y$-axis by factor 3, followed by a translation $(0,-2)$.
a. Give the formula of $g$ (expanded).
b. Find the vertex of the graph of $g$ in two ways: from the formula, and by transforming the vertex of $f$.

**20.** The number of downloads (in thousands) of an app $t$ weeks after launch is modelled by $N(t)=\dfrac{1200t}{t+6}$, $t\ge0$.
a. Which value does $N$ approach in the long run? Explain.
b. Bereken exact from which moment $N>900$.
c. Write $t$ as a function of $N$.

## Challenge

**21.** Given $f(x)=\dfrac{ax+b}{cx+d}$ with $c \neq 0$ and $ad-bc\neq0$.
a. Find $f^{-1}$.
b. Prove that $f$ is its own inverse ($f^{-1}=f$) if $d=-a$.
c. Why is the condition $ad-bc\neq 0$ needed?

**22.** Solve: $\sqrt{x+3-4\sqrt{x-1}} + \sqrt{x+8-6\sqrt{x-1}} = 1$.
*Hint:* substitute $t=\sqrt{x-1}$.

**23.** Given $g(x)=\lvert x^2-4\lvert x\rvert +3\rvert$. Find, for every real number $k$, the number of solutions of $g(x)=k$.

**24.** Let $f$ be a strictly increasing function with inverse $f^{-1}$.
a. Prove: if the graphs of $f$ and $f^{-1}$ meet at $(a,b)$, then $a=b$.
b. Give an example of a decreasing function $f$ whose graph meets the graph of $f^{-1}$ at a point not on the line $y=x$.

**25.** A function $f$ defined for all $x\neq 0$ satisfies $f(x)+2f\left(\tfrac1x\right) = 3x$ for all $x \neq 0$.
a. Find $f(x)$.
b. Solve $f(x) = f(-x)$.
