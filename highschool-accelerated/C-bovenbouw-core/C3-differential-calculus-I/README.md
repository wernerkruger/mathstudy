# C3 Differential calculus I

[← Stage C overview](../README.md) · Previous: [C2 Exponential and logarithmic functions](../C2-exponential-logarithmic/) · Next: [C4 Trigonometric functions](../C4-trigonometric-functions/)

**Stage:** C, upper-school core · **School year:** klas 2 (2028–29) · **Length:** 10 weeks at about 4 hours a week

## Prerequisites

- [C1 Functions, graphs and equations](../C1-functions-graphs/): power functions, rational functions, solving equations and inequalities exactly, sign diagrams, asymptotes and informal limits.
- [B2 Algebra and quadratics](../../B-onderbouw-compact/B2-algebra-quadratics/): expanding $(a+b)^n$ for small $n$, factorising, the quadratic formula.
- [B1 Linear relations and equations](../../B-onderbouw-compact/B1-linear-relations/): slope and equation of a line.

C2 is not needed for this module, apart from general fluency.

## Objectives

By the end of this module he can:

1. Compute the average rate of change (*gemiddelde verandering*, *differentiequotiënt* $\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x}$) on an interval and interpret it as the slope of a secant line.
2. Explain the instantaneous rate of change (*differentiaalquotiënt* $\frac{dy}{dx}$) as the limit of difference quotients, using informal limits (*limieten*).
3. Compute derivatives (*afgeleide functies*) from the definition $f'(x)=\lim\limits_{h\to0}\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}$ for polynomials, $\frac1x$ and $\sqrt x$.
4. State and **prove** the power rule for positive integer exponents, the sum and constant-multiple rules, the product rule (*productregel*) and the quotient rule (*quotiëntregel*); use the power rule for all rational exponents.
5. Find the equation of a tangent line (*raaklijn*) at a point, a tangent line with a given slope, and tangent lines through a point not on the graph.
6. Use the sign of $f'$ to find where $f$ is increasing or decreasing (*stijgend, dalend*), and find extreme values (*extreme waarden*: *maximum, minimum*) with a sign diagram of $f'$.
7. Solve optimisation problems (*optimaliseringsproblemen*) in contexts.
8. Use the second derivative (*tweede afgeleide*) for concavity (*toenemend / afnemend stijgend*, convex/concave) and find inflection points (*buigpunten*) and the tangent at an inflection point (*buigraaklijn*).

The chain rule (*kettingregel*) and the derivatives of $e^x$, $\ln x$, $\sin x$ and $\cos x$ are **not** in this module; they come in stage D, module [D1 Differential calculus II](../../D-bovenbouw-advanced/D1-differential-calculus-II/). All problems here avoid them.

B3 functies en grafieken for graph analysis.
- **Wiskunde techniek (concept 2026):** A5 veranderingen (rate of change, derivative, extremes, optimisation), B3 redeneren en bewijzen (proving the rules), B1 probleemaanpakken (optimisation).

- [ ] compute a difference quotient and explain it as the slope of a secant line
- [ ] explain in words what $f'(3)=5$ means for a graph and for a context
- [ ] differentiate $x^2$, $x^3$, $\frac1x$ and $\sqrt x$ from the definition
- [ ] prove the power rule for positive integers $n$ and the product rule
- [ ] derive the quotient rule
- [ ] differentiate polynomials, powers with rational exponents, products and quotients quickly and correctly
- [ ] find a tangent line at a point, with a given slope, and through an outside point
- [ ] find extreme values exactly with a sign diagram of $f'$ and say whether each is a maximum or minimum

- [ ] find inflection points with $f''$ and explain why $f''(a)=0$ alone is not enough
- [ ] write a CE "toon aan" answer involving derivatives
