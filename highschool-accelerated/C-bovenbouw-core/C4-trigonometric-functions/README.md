# C4 Trigonometric functions

[← Stage C overview](../README.md) · Previous: [C3 Differential calculus I](../C3-differential-calculus-I/) · Next stage: [D bovenbouw advanced](../../D-bovenbouw-advanced/)

**Stage:** C, upper-school core · **School year:** klas 2 (2028–29) · **Length:** 8 weeks at about 4 hours a week

## Prerequisites

- [B3 Geometry: Pythagoras, similarity, trigonometry, proof](../../B-onderbouw-compact/B3-geometry-trigonometry/): sine, cosine and tangent in right triangles (*SOS-CAS-TOA*), Pythagoras, special triangles.
- [C1 Functions, graphs and equations](../C1-functions-graphs/): transformations (needed for $a+b\sin(c(x-d))$), inverse functions, solving equations exactly.

C2 and C3 are not needed; C4 can be done before C3 if that suits the planning.

## Objectives

By the end of this module he can:

1. Convert between degrees and radians (*radialen*) and explain why radians are the natural unit; compute arc length (*booglengte*) and sector area.
2. Define $\sin$, $\cos$ and $\tan$ for every real number using the unit circle (*eenheidscirkel*).
3. Give exact values (*exacte waarden*) at multiples of $\frac\pi6$ and $\frac\pi4$, using the unit circle and symmetry.
4. Sketch $y=\sin x$, $y=\cos x$, $y=\tan x$ and transformed sine functions $y=a+b\sin(c(x-d))$; read off and use the period (*periode*), amplitude (*amplitude*), equilibrium (*evenwichtsstand*) and starting point (*beginpunt*); find a formula from a graph or context.
5. Solve trigonometric equations (*goniometrische vergelijkingen*) exactly, with all solutions ($+k\cdot2\pi$, $+k\cdot\pi$), and select those in an interval.
6. Prove and use the identities $\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1$, the addition formulas (*somformules / verschilformules*) and the double-angle formulas (*verdubbelingsformules*).
7. Prove and use the sine rule (*sinusregel*) and the cosine rule (*cosinusregel*) in any triangle, and the area formula $\tfrac12ab\sin\gamma$.

## Exam mapping

- **Wiskunde B (CvTE syllabus 2028):** domain D Goniometrische functies (unit circle, radians, graphs, exact values, equations, formulas such as $\sin^2+\cos^2=1$, sum and double-angle formulas from the formula sheet). The sine and cosine rules belong to domain E1 meetkundige vaardigheden (Meetkunde met coördinaten).
- **Wiskunde techniek (concept 2026):** A4 verbanden (goniometric functions as standard functions, periodic models), A6 meetkunde (sine and cosine rules, angles), B3 redeneren en bewijzen.

## I can…

- [ ] convert between degrees and radians and compute arc length and sector area
- [ ] explain $\sin t$ and $\cos t$ as coordinates of a point on the unit circle
- [ ] give exact values of $\sin$, $\cos$, $\tan$ at all multiples of $\frac\pi6$ and $\frac\pi4$ without a calculator
- [ ] sketch $y=a+b\sin(c(x-d))$ and read off equilibrium, amplitude, period and starting point
- [ ] find a sinusoid formula from a graph or a context (tides, Ferris wheel, daylight)
- [ ] solve $\sin A=\sin B$, $\cos A=\cos B$ and $\tan A=\tan B$ with all solutions
- [ ] solve equations that need an identity, such as $\sin x=\cos2x$
- [ ] prove $\sin^2x+\cos^2x=1$ and the formula for $\cos(\alpha-\beta)$, and derive the other addition and double-angle formulas from it
- [ ] prove and use the sine rule and the cosine rule

## Exit criteria

- At least 80% of the Practice and CE-level problems solved correctly without the solutions.
- At least two Challenge problems solved, including problem 22 (proof of the addition formula).
- He can solve a goniometric equation of CE level with all solutions, and select the solutions in a given interval, without errors in three consecutive attempts.
- He can do two past CE questions on goniometric functions (without derivatives) within exam time.
