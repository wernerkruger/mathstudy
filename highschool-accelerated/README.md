# Accelerated secondary track: groep 8 to VWO 6

[← Back to all tracks](../README.md)

For a mathematically gifted 12-year-old in groep 8 (school year 2026–27) who will go to gymnasium. The track covers the whole VWO mathematics programme at the highest level, years ahead of school, and then goes beyond it towards university mathematics. A competition track runs alongside throughout.

Material is in English with the Dutch school and exam terms alongside. See the [glossary](GLOSSARY.md).

## What "the highest level" means for this child

Today the highest VWO mathematics is **Wiskunde B** (central exam) together with **Wiskunde D** (school exam only). Both are being replaced:

- New VWO exam programmes start in klas 4 in September 2029. This child reaches klas 4 in September 2030, so he will be taught and examined under the new programmes.
- **Wiskunde techniek** replaces Wiskunde B for the Natuur en Techniek profile. It keeps the Wiskunde B core (functions, calculus, trigonometry, vectors) and adds probability in the central exam, differential equations, parametric curves, algorithms and geometric proof.
- **Wiskunde D** is being rewritten under a new name; final programmes are due in September 2029.

This track therefore covers **everything in current Wiskunde B, the additions in wiskunde techniek, and current Wiskunde D** (probability and statistics, dynamical systems, conics and geometry, complex numbers). Whatever the final programmes look like, he will have covered them.

Sources: SLO (actualisatie-examenprogrammas.nl, September 2026) and the CvTE Wiskunde B syllabus 2028. Re-check the wiskunde techniek and Wiskunde D syllabi each year.

## Time

The plan assumes **3–4 hours a week** of study on top of school, for about 38 weeks a year. Schedules count in "weeks" of about 4 hours. The competition track adds about 1 hour a week from klas 1.

He should not do this alone. Read the [parent guide](PARENT_GUIDE.md), including how to agree acceleration with the school (compacting, a plusklas, early exams).

By the end of stage E (klas 4) he will have covered the full upper-school programme. Under Dutch law a school may let a pupil sit the central exam in a subject one or two years early (klas 4 or klas 5 in VWO). Because the first central exam under the new programme is probably in May 2032, **klas 5 (May 2032) is the realistic early-exam date**. Discuss this with the school in klas 4.

## What each module contains

Every module folder has:

- `README.md`: objectives (with the Dutch terms), which school year and exam domain it corresponds to, resources, and a week-by-week plan.
- `notes.md`: explanations written for a strong young learner, with worked examples, the reasons things are true, and proofs where they are within reach.
- `problems.md`: graded problems: practice, school-exam level, and challenge.
- `solutions.md`: full worked solutions.
